1 · 什麼是心理側寫？

不同於前 5 章針對「未知兇手」的側寫，心理側寫聚焦於「已知個體」，目的是預測其「潛在行為」 — 多用於危險性與適任性評估。

Canter & Alison (2000) 強調：心理側寫本質上是「傳統心理測驗與評估程序的延伸」。因此應由具訓練的心理／精神專業人員執行。

心理側寫也有「正面用途」：

  • 警察候選人甄選、升遷適任性評估
  • 政治人物（含總統候選人）特質研究
  • 領導與職場潛能評估

但本章聚焦最被研究的應用：預防危險 — 威脅評估與風險評估。

1 · What Is Psychological Profiling?

Unlike the previous chapters' work on unknown offenders, psychological profiling targets a known individual, predicting their potential behavior — mostly for dangerousness and fitness.

Canter & Alison (2000) stress: psychological profiling is essentially "an offshoot of traditional psychological testing and assessment," requiring trained mental-health professionals.

It has positive uses too:

  • Police candidate selection and promotion fitness
  • Personality research on political (even presidential) candidates
  • Leadership and workplace potential assessment

This chapter focuses on the most researched application: preventing harm — threat and risk assessment.

2 · Threat vs Risk — 兩種程序的差別

🎯 Threat Assessment
  • ● 起點：「已表達的具體威脅」
  • ● 目標：評估威脅執行可信度
  • ● 對象：可能不明（如恐嚇信、社群貼文）
  • ● 例：校園、職場、政治人物威脅
⚖️ Risk Assessment
  • ● 起點：「無特定威脅」
  • ● 目標：預測未來暴力機率
  • ● 對象：已知個體
  • ● 例：保釋／假釋／監禁／民事拘留決定

兩者都是「機率任務」，都需實證基礎；委託方通常想要的不只是統計數字，還包括潛在後果的估計 + 緩解方法（Hanson 2009）。

2 · Threat vs Risk

🎯 Threat Assessment
  • ● Trigger: an explicit threat
  • ● Goal: gauge credibility of the threat being carried out
  • ● Source: may be unknown (letters, social posts)
  • ● E.g., school, workplace, threats to public figures
⚖️ Risk Assessment
  • ● Trigger: no specific threat
  • ● Goal: predict probability of future violence
  • ● Target: known individual
  • ● E.g., bail / parole / commitment decisions

Both are probabilistic; both need empirical grounding. Requesters want more than statistics — also potential consequences and mitigation (Hanson 2009).

3 · 歷史 — Hitler 心理側寫

二戰期間，美國 OSS（戰略情報局）委託心理學家 Walter Langer 撰寫一份 281 頁的 Hitler 心理側寫（1943）。Langer 採 Freudian / 精神分析 取徑，內容包括：

  • Hitler 的伊底帕斯情結、母親過度潔癖對其上廁所訓練的影響
  • 童年目睹父母性行為、食物相關的快感與厭惡
  • 「Germany 是 Hitler 理想母親的象徵」

同期 Harvard 心理學家 Henry Murray 也製作了一份 240 頁版本 — 正確預測 Hitler 在戰爭結束前會自殺。

⚠️ 政治人物側寫的風險：1993 年 CIA 誤告國會 Haiti 流亡總統 Aristide「易患躁鬱」 — 一位參議員竟在參議院稱他「psychopath」。提醒我們：政治人物的心理側寫高度敏感且易被誤用。

3 · History — Profiling Hitler

During WWII the U.S. OSS commissioned psychologist Walter Langer to write a 281-page psychological profile of Hitler (1943). Langer used a Freudian / psychoanalytic lens, including:

  • Hitler's Oedipal conflict; his mother's excessive tidiness shaping his toilet training
  • Childhood witnessing of parental sex; food-related pleasures and revulsions
  • "Germany as a symbol of Hitler's ideal mother"

Harvard psychologist Henry Murray's parallel 240-page version correctly predicted Hitler's suicide before the war's end.

⚠️ Risks of profiling public figures: In 1993 the CIA wrongly told Congress that exiled Haitian president Aristide was prone to manic depression — a senator then called him a "psychopath" on the Senate floor. Public-figure profiling is sensitive and easily abused.

4 · 校園槍擊與 Safe School Initiative

4.1 Columbine 1999 — 一個文化腳本的誕生

1999 年 Columbine 高中槍擊（15 死 23 傷）成為 1990 年代第二大報導事件。其影響不僅在於數字 — 它為後續校槍犯創造了「文化腳本」：

  • 留下大量影片、相片、日記、原始部落格頁面 — 形塑「校槍品牌」（Kiilakoski & Oksanen, 2011）。
  • Columbine 後 12 件美國校槍中 2/3 明顯模仿；甚至跨國（如 2007 芬蘭 Auvinen）連音樂選擇都相同。
  • Columbine 前的校槍多為「個人申冤」（被分手、被霸凌復仇）；之後出現「為其他社會邊緣人發聲」的新意識形態。

4.2 SSI 十大關鍵發現

美國 Secret Service + Department of Education 合作的 Safe School Initiative（2002）分析 1974–2000 年 37 件 41 名攻擊者，提出 10 大發現：

#發現
1校槍很少是衝動行為 — 多事先計畫 1 天到 1 年。
2≥3/4 案例事前至少 1 人知情（leakage）；93% 是同儕，鮮少成人。
383% 攻擊者不會直接通知目標。
493% 攻擊者事前出現引人擔心的行為。
5多數難以應付重大失落或失敗；多有自殺念頭或嘗試。
6多覺得被霸凌、迫害、傷害。
7~2/3 案前已有使用武器經驗。
844% 受他人影響或慫恿。
975% 事件由師生／自停制止，非執法干預；過半 ≤ 15 分鐘。
10「沒有準確或可用的學生槍手側寫」
🎯 SSI 替代建議：以「事實導向的威脅評估（fact-based threat assessment）」取代 profile — 看學生「說什麼、做什麼」，而非「看起來像不像過去的兇手」。

4.3 心理共通特徵（Leary 等 2003）

15 件校槍研究：peer rejection 是核心，但需搭配至少 1 個額外因素：

  • 心理問題：低衝動控制、缺乏同理、嚴重憂鬱、攻擊性、反社會行為
  • 對槍械／爆裂物的興趣
  • 對死亡的病態著迷（如 Jeffrey Weise 案）

4 · School Shootings & the Safe School Initiative

4.1 Columbine 1999 — Birth of a Cultural Script

The 1999 Columbine shooting (15 dead, 23 wounded) was the 1990s' second-most covered news story. Its lasting impact: a "cultural script" for future school shooters.

  • Left abundant videos, photos, diaries, proto-blogs — "branding" school shootings (Kiilakoski & Oksanen, 2011).
  • 2/3 of the 12 post-Columbine U.S. shootings clearly mimicked Columbine; even cross-national cases (2007 Finland's Auvinen) reused the same music.
  • Pre-Columbine shootings were personal grievances (breakups, bullying revenge); after, came ideological "speaking for outcasts."

4.2 SSI's Ten Findings

The U.S. Secret Service + Department of Education Safe School Initiative (2002) analyzed 37 incidents / 41 attackers from 1974–2000:

#Finding
1Rarely sudden/impulsive — planned 1 day to a year.
2≥3/4 cases had at least one person who knew beforehand (leakage); 93% peers, rarely adults.
383% of attackers did NOT warn the targets directly.
493% showed concerning behavior beforehand.
5Most struggled with significant losses or failures; many had suicidal ideation/attempts.
6Most felt bullied, persecuted, harmed.
7~2/3 had prior weapon experience.
844% were influenced or encouraged by others.
975% stopped by school staff/peers, not law enforcement; over half lasted ≤15 minutes.
10"No accurate or useful profile of students."
🎯 SSI's alternative: "Fact-based threat assessment" — focused on what students say and do, not whether they "look like" past shooters.

4.3 Common Psychological Patterns (Leary et al. 2003)

15-case study: peer rejection is central, but must combine with at least one of:

  • Psychological issues: low impulse control, low empathy, severe depression, aggression, antisocial behavior
  • Interest in firearms / explosives
  • Morbid fascination with death (e.g., Jeffrey Weise)

5 · Substantive vs Transient — 哪些威脅該認真看待？

Cornell 等 (2004) 研究 188 件學生威脅，把威脅分兩類：

💨 Transient 短暫威脅

沒有持續傷害意圖；情緒化（如氣話、衝突當下），通常一道歉就能化解。多為一般紀律問題。

🚨 Substantive 實質威脅

持續傷害意圖，O'Toole (2000) 五大指標：

  1. 具體計畫細節（受害者、武器、時間、地點）
  2. 威脅在時間上重複，或被傳給多名同儕
  3. 有計畫執行的證據
  4. 已招募同夥或邀請觀眾
  5. 已實際取得槍械、炸藥材料、彈藥
📌 注意：情緒化用詞（「你毀了我的生活！！！」）未被驗證為可信度指標 — 只是表達當下情緒。Cornell 樣本中 188 件僅 3 件嚴重到被開除；50% 被停學 1–10 天。

Cornell 等發展 Virginia Student Threat Assessment Guidelines（145 頁手冊）為 K-12 提供 step-by-step 決策樹。

5 · Substantive vs Transient Threats

Cornell et al. (2004) studied 188 student threats:

💨 Transient

No lasting intent; emotional outburst, usually resolved by apology. A routine discipline matter.

🚨 Substantive

Sustained intent. O'Toole's (2000) five indicators:

  1. Specific plan details (victim, weapon, time, place)
  2. Threat repeated over time or spread among peers
  3. Evidence of planning to carry it out
  4. Accomplices recruited or audience invited
  5. Actual acquisition of firearms, bomb materials, ammunition
📌 Note: Emotional content ("You ruined my life!!!") has NOT been validated as a credibility indicator. Of Cornell's 188 cases, only 3 led to expulsion; 50% had 1–10 day suspensions.

Cornell developed the Virginia Student Threat Assessment Guidelines — a 145-page step-by-step manual for K-12.

6 · 職場暴力（Workplace Violence）

「Going postal」一詞讓人誤以為同事互殺是主流。實際上：

  • 美國每年職場命案約 564 件，是職業致死第二大原因。
  • 搶劫犯占 40%；同事 / 前同事僅 4–7%；多數受害者為女性（前台、HR、家暴外溢）。

四類分類（California Division of Occupational Safety and Health, 1995）

I

Type I 無合法關係

外來犯如搶劫便利商店、速食店；最多。

II

Type II 服務接受者

病患、學生、客戶、案主；針對護士、社工、教師、治療師。

III

Type III 現／前任員工

被解雇、降職、待遇不公而報復；本章重點。

IV

Type IV 家暴外溢

無職場關係但與員工有親密關係 — 家暴擴散到工作場所。

Type III 的個人預測因子（Barling 1996）

  1. 酒精使用 — 「潛在暴力員工的側寫中酒精消耗量過高」是典型描述。
  2. 過去攻擊與暴力史 — 過去行為仍是最佳預測因子。
  3. 自尊低落 — 任何打擊自尊的事件可能讓他「越線」。
  4. 職場心理攻擊 — 嘲弄、騷擾、羞辱（如 Pierre Lebrun 因口吃被嘲笑後殺害 4 名同事）。

Dupré 等 (2010) 加上 Interpersonal Injustice（人際不公） — 員工感受到被不尊重、不公平對待，會引發強烈報復情緒。

威脅評估的 5 大檢查點

  1. 威脅本身的性質與脈絡
  2. 被指認的目標
  3. 嫌犯的動機
  4. 嫌犯執行的能力（含武器持有）
  5. 嫌犯背景（含過去暴力、職場行為）

6 · Workplace Violence

"Going postal" misleads — coworker-on-coworker violence is the minority:

  • U.S. averages ~564 workplace homicides/year — 2nd leading occupational cause.
  • Robbers ~40%; coworkers / former coworkers only 4–7%; women disproportionately victims (front-desk, HR, domestic spillover).

Four Categories (CA Division of OSH, 1995)

I

Type I — No Connection

External offender (store robbery, fast-food); most common.

II

Type II — Service Recipient

Patient, student, client; against nurses, social workers, teachers, therapists.

III

Type III — Current/Former Employee

Fired, demoted, treated unfairly; this chapter's focus.

IV

Type IV — Domestic Spillover

No workplace tie but personal/intimate relationship with an employee.

Personal Predictors of Type III (Barling, 1996)

  1. Alcohol use — "profiles of potentially violent employees emphasize their excessive alcohol consumption."
  2. Aggression / violence history — past behavior remains the best predictor.
  3. Low self-esteem — any blow may "push them over the edge."
  4. Workplace psychological aggression — taunting, harassment (Pierre Lebrun, mocked for stuttering, killed 4 coworkers).

Dupré et al. (2010) add Interpersonal Injustice — perceived disrespect/unfairness fuels retaliation.

Five Checkpoints for Workplace Threat Assessment

  1. Nature and context of the threat
  2. Identified target
  3. Suspect's motivation
  4. Capacity to act (weapon access)
  5. Background (prior violence, work history)

7 · 風險評估與 SPJ 模型

7.1 三大評估取徑

📊 Actuarial

純統計、量化風險因子（VRAG、HCR-20、LSI-R、STATIC-99、SORAG、ODARA、DVRAG）。Grove & Meehl (1996)：50 年研究顯示 actuarial 多數情況「優於」純臨床。

🩺 Clinical

仰賴臨床經驗、判斷、訪談。被批為過往「錯比對多」 — Hanson (2009)：早期 forensic 預測「錯了兩倍於對」。

🧠 Structured Professional Judgment (SPJ)

把暴力視為「情境動態連續性」建構；以實證 actuarial 風險因子為起點，再以結構化臨床判斷考慮個案脈絡。McGowan 等 (2011)：「混合實證知識與臨床判斷，引導個體傾向 + 環境誘發因子的評估。」

7.2 Static vs Dynamic 風險因子

類型是否可變例子
Static不可變、歷史性父母犯罪史、自身少年期反社會行為起點
Dynamic — Stable緩慢可變（月／年）對暴力色情的態度、長期與偏差同儕關係
Dynamic — Acute快速波動（小時／日）情緒、酒精、藥物

Hanson & Harris (2000)：對性犯罪再犯而言，Acute 預測力 > Stable。McEwan 等 (2011)：「最有效的暴力介入針對與個體因果相關的 dynamic 風險因子。」

7 · Risk Assessment & the SPJ Model

7.1 Three Approaches

📊 Actuarial

Purely statistical, quantified risk factors (VRAG, HCR-20, LSI-R, STATIC-99, SORAG, ODARA, DVRAG). Grove & Meehl (1996): 50 years of research show actuarial usually beats clinical.

🩺 Clinical

Relies on clinical experience, interviews, judgment. Historically wrong more than right — Hanson (2009): forensic predictors were wrong "twice as often as right."

🧠 Structured Professional Judgment (SPJ)

Views violence as a contextual, dynamic construct; starts from empirically validated risk factors, then adds structured clinical judgment for case context. McGowan et al. (2011): "blends empirical knowledge and clinical judgment to guide assessment of individual predisposition + ecological triggers."

7.2 Static vs Dynamic Risk Factors

TypeChangeable?Examples
StaticNo (historical)Parental criminality; one's own early antisocial onset
Dynamic — StableSlowly (months/years)Attitudes toward violent pornography; long-term deviant peers
Dynamic — AcuteFast (hours/days)Mood, alcohol, drugs

Hanson & Harris (2000): for sex-offender reoffense, Acute > Stable. McEwan et al. (2011): "Effective interventions target dynamic risk factors causally tied to violence for that individual."

8 · 跟蹤（Stalking）

8.1 定義與盛行率

Sheridan & Roberts (2011)：「持續、不被歡迎的接觸與通訊，造成受害者恐懼或痛苦。」常包括：徘徊、跟隨、監視、多次來電、散布謠言、毀壞個人物品、騷擾家人朋友、寄威脅或性暗示「禮物」、攻擊行為。

  • 80% 受害者為女性；多數加害者為男性。
  • 終身被跟蹤盛行率 5–16%。Tjaden & Thoennes (1998b)：女性 8%、男性 2%。
  • 長期跟蹤總體暴力率 32%、性暴力率 12%（Spitzberg 2007）。
  • 1989 女演員 Rebecca Schaeffer 被迷戀粉絲殺害觸發加州首部反跟蹤法；現 50 州 + DC + 加拿大皆有。

8.2 暴力預測因子

Rosenfeld 與其團隊（2002, 2004, 2009）：

  • Intimacy effect：曾為親密關係者最危險（74% 暴力），陌生人最低（2%）。
  • 過去威脅紀錄是強預測因子。
  • 武器持有 / 使用紀錄。
  • 頻繁出現於受害者家／工作地：Sheridan & Roberts (2011) 發現「每週 ≥ 3 次」者最危險，「在受害者財產上紮營」者極危險。
  • 跟蹤行為頻率／強度上升是重要警訊。
  • 多數情況下，嚴重精神疾患「不」是強預測因子。

8 · Stalking

8.1 Definition & Prevalence

Sheridan & Roberts (2011): "persistent unwanted contact/communication causing fear or distress." Often includes: loitering, following, surveillance, repeated calls, rumor spreading, property destruction, harassing family/friends, sending threatening/sexual "gifts," assaults.

  • 80% of victims are women; perpetrators mostly men.
  • Lifetime prevalence 5–16%. Tjaden & Thoennes (1998b): women 8%, men 2%.
  • Long-term stalking: 32% overall violence, 12% sexual violence (Spitzberg 2007).
  • The 1989 murder of actress Rebecca Schaeffer triggered California's first anti-stalking law; now all 50 states + DC + Canada.

8.2 Violence Predictors

Rosenfeld and colleagues (2002, 2004, 2009):

  • Intimacy effect: former intimates are most dangerous (74% violence) vs strangers (2%).
  • Prior threats are a strong predictor.
  • Weapon access / history.
  • Frequent visits to victim's home/work: Sheridan & Roberts (2011) — ≥3 times/week signals high risk; "camping" on property is extremely dangerous.
  • Escalating frequency/severity is critical.
  • Severe mental disorder is generally NOT a strong predictor.

9 · 跟蹤者分類

9.1 RECON（Mohandie 等, 2006）

基於 1,000+ 名跟蹤者；用「RElationship + CONtext」兩維度：

Type I 有過往關係Type II 無過往關係
對私人Intimate（前親密伴侶） — 74% 暴力，最高再犯Private Stranger — 心理疾患多，自殺風險高
對公眾人物Acquaintance（朋友、同事） — 1/3 攻擊或破壞Public Figure — 27% 為女性；多疑似精神疾患；2% 暴力（多有保全保護）

Eke 等 (2011) 9 年後追蹤：3/4 再犯，56% 為跟蹤相關；超過半數 3 個月內再犯。

9.2 Mullen 等五類

  1. Intimacy-seeking：社交孤立、寂寞、對「真愛」抱不切實際期待。
  2. Rejected：多為前伴侶；同時尋求和解與報復；最大群組；多有人格疾患；最易實際攻擊。
  3. Incompetent：有跟蹤前科；視受害者為「合適伴侶」但不至迷戀。
  4. Resentful：動機為使對方恐懼困擾；多偏執妄想；威脅多於實際暴力。
  5. Predatory：小群但具高度性暴力風險；享受跟蹤與性侵的權力感。

9.3 SPJ 跟蹤工具：SAM 與 SRP

Kropp 等 (2011) 認為，因 actuarial 工具不足，跟蹤評估應採 SPJ：

  • SAM（Stalking Assessment and Management，加拿大）— 三領域：跟蹤行為型態、加害者心理／背景、受害者脆弱性。
  • SRP（Stalking Risk Profile，澳洲）— 基於 Mullen 動機 typology；建議搭配 HCR-20、SARA 等工具。

兩者允許臨床者最終評為「低／中／高」風險；非要取代專業判斷。

9.4 跟蹤通常如何結束？

Tjaden & Thoennes 調查：

  • 18% — 跟蹤者找到新對象
  • 15% — 警方警告（非逮捕）
  • 正式介入（逮捕、判決、保護令）不必然有效，有時反而激怒跟蹤者
  • 對嚴重案件，受害者「搬離且保密行蹤」可能是最有效手段

9 · Stalker Typologies

9.1 RECON (Mohandie et al., 2006)

Based on 1,000+ stalkers; built on RElationship + CONtext:

Type I — Prior relationshipType II — No prior
Private targetIntimate (former partner) — 74% violent; highest recidivismPrivate Stranger — high psychiatric comorbidity, suicide risk
Public targetAcquaintance (friend, coworker) — 1/3 assault or damagePublic Figure — 27% female; many psychiatric; only 2% violent (security helps)

Eke et al. (2011) 9-year follow-up: 3/4 reoffended, 56% in stalking; over half within 3 months.

9.2 Mullen et al.'s Five

  1. Intimacy-seeking: socially isolated, lonely, unrealistic "true love" beliefs.
  2. Rejected: often former partners; mixes reconciliation and revenge; the largest group; often personality-disordered; most likely to assault.
  3. Incompetent: prior stalking history; sees victim as suitable but not obsessed.
  4. Resentful: motive is to frighten/distress; paranoid/delusional; threatens more than assaults.
  5. Predatory: small but highly sexually violent; enjoys the power of stalking and assault.

9.3 SPJ Stalking Tools: SAM & SRP

Kropp et al. (2011): with no good actuarial tool, stalking risk needs SPJ.

  • SAM (Stalking Assessment and Management, Canada) — three domains: stalking patterns, perpetrator psychology/background, victim vulnerability.
  • SRP (Stalking Risk Profile, Australia) — rooted in Mullen's motivational typology; recommends pairing with HCR-20, SARA, etc.

Both allow clinicians to rate risk low/moderate/high; not meant to replace professional judgment.

9.4 How Does Stalking Usually End?

Tjaden & Thoennes survey:

  • 18% — stalker finds a new target
  • 15% — police warning (not arrest)
  • Formal interventions (arrest, conviction, restraining orders) are not reliably effective — sometimes antagonize
  • For severe cases, the victim "relocating and concealing whereabouts" is often most effective

11 · 本章總結

  1. 心理側寫聚焦「已知個體」的危險性與適任性 — 應由受訓心理／精神專業人員執行。
  2. Threat（已表達威脅，看可信度） vs Risk（無特定威脅，看未來暴力機率）。
  3. Columbine 創造校槍「文化腳本」；SSI 結論「沒有準確的學生槍手側寫」 — 改用「事實導向威脅評估」。
  4. Cornell 把學生威脅分 Transient vs Substantive；後者五大指標：細節、重複、計畫證據、招募同夥、實際取得武器。
  5. 職場暴力四類；Type III 個人預測因子：酒精、暴力史、低自尊、職場心理攻擊 + interpersonal injustice。
  6. Actuarial vs Clinical 50 年論戰由 SPJ 模型整合；風險因子分 Static / Stable Dynamic / Acute Dynamic。
  7. 跟蹤盛行率 5–16%；intimacy effect 是核心；RECON 與 Mullen 兩個 typology 互補；SAM/SRP 是 SPJ 跟蹤工具。
  8. 跟蹤通常因「跟蹤者轉移注意」或「警方警告」停止；正式介入有時反而激怒；嚴重情況可能需搬離保密。
🎯 一句話帶走：「沒有完美的 profile，只有越來越好的問題：他做了什麼？說了什麼？以前怎麼做的？此刻能怎麼緩解？」

11 · Chapter Summary

  1. Psychological profiling targets the dangerousness/fitness of a known individual — by trained mental-health professionals.
  2. Threat (explicit, evaluate credibility) vs Risk (no explicit threat, predict future violence).
  3. Columbine created the school-shooter "cultural script"; SSI concluded "no accurate student shooter profile" — use fact-based threat assessment instead.
  4. Cornell sorts student threats into Transient vs Substantive; the five Substantive markers: detail, repetition, planning evidence, accomplices, actual weapon acquisition.
  5. Four workplace violence types; Type III personal predictors: alcohol, violence history, low self-esteem, workplace psychological aggression + interpersonal injustice.
  6. The 50-year actuarial-vs-clinical debate is resolved by SPJ; factors split into Static / Stable Dynamic / Acute Dynamic.
  7. Stalking prevalence 5–16%; the intimacy effect is central; RECON and Mullen typologies complement; SAM/SRP are SPJ stalking tools.
  8. Stalking usually ends when the stalker shifts targets or police warn; formal intervention may backfire; severe cases may need relocation.
🎯 One-liner takeaway: "There's no perfect profile — only better questions: What did they do? Say? Done before? How can we mitigate now?"
