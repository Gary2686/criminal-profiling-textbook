1 · 兒童綁架

1.1 兩種定義 — 法律 vs 媒體典型

Finkelhor 等 (1992) 區分：

  • 法律定義：任何非法帶走或挾持未成年人 — 範圍極廣（含非監護方父母帶走、14 歲少女隨 20 歲男友離家）。
  • 媒體典型（stereotypical abduction, SA）：陌生人作案 + 至少一項：過夜消失 / 帶走 50+ 英里 / 殺害 / 勒贖 / 永久扣押意圖。

1.2 數字告訴我們什麼

  • 1988：~200–300 件 SA；1999：~115 件 SA（Finkelhor 2002）。
  • 家庭內綁架：每年 ~200,000 件；非家庭：~58,000 件；登記性犯罪者僅占 <1% 的兒童綁架（2010）。
  • SA 中 40% 受害者死亡、4% 失蹤；74% 被殺害者死於 3 小時內、44% 死於 1 小時內（Hanfland 1997）。
  • 「24 小時」警語其實是「若被殺害，通常在 24 小時內」 — 不是「24 小時後就會死」。

1.3 加害者輪廓（Hanfland 等 1997；Beyer & Beasley 2003）

  • 98% 男性，平均 27 歲，多數 < 30 歲。
  • 85% 未婚；50% 獨居或與父母同住；社交技巧差、人際關係少。
  • 多為高中以下學歷、失業或粗工。
  • 頻繁搬家、與社區聯繫薄弱、常被形容「奇怪」、酒精/藥物問題常見。
  • ~67% 跨案 MO 一致（高於一般連續殺人犯） → 對 case linkage 較有利。
  • ~2/3 過去有暴力前科，多為對兒童的性侵 → 警方應優先比對已知名冊。
AMBER Alert & CARD

AMBER Alert 由 1996 年 Amber Hagerman 案啟發；50 州皆有。但 Griffin & Miller (2008) 提醒：「慢回應不一定致死 — 是兇手意圖殺害本身使任何回應都太慢」。Gier 等 (2012) 建議公告時提供「整潔」與「凌亂」兩種照片，提升公眾辨識率。

CARD（Child Abduction Rapid Deployment）團隊 — FBI 自 2006 年部署，2007 年底 26 次部署中 42% 兒童生還、50% 死亡、8% 仍失蹤。

1 · Child Abduction

1.1 Two Definitions — Legal vs Stereotypical

Finkelhor et al. (1992) distinguish:

  • Legal: any unlawful taking or holding of a minor — broad (includes non-custodial parent, 14-year-old leaving with 20-year-old boyfriend).
  • Stereotypical abduction (SA): stranger-perpetrated AND at least one of: overnight / 50+ miles / killed / ransomed / intent to keep permanently.

1.2 What the Numbers Tell Us

  • 1988: ~200–300 SAs; 1999: ~115 SAs (Finkelhor 2002).
  • Family abductions: ~200,000/year; nonfamily: ~58,000; registered sex offenders only <1% of child abductions (2010).
  • 40% of SA victims killed; 4% still missing; 74% die within 3 hours, 44% within 1 hour (Hanfland 1997).
  • The "24-hour" caution actually means "if killed, usually within 24 hours" — not "will die after 24 hours."

1.3 Offender Profile (Hanfland et al. 1997; Beyer & Beasley 2003)

  • 98% male, mean age 27, most <30.
  • 85% unmarried; 50% live alone or with parents; poor social skills, few friends.
  • Mostly less than high school; unemployed or unskilled labor.
  • Move often; weak community ties; often described "strange"; alcohol/drug problems common.
  • ~67% use the same MO across crimes (higher than typical serial killers) → favorable for linkage.
  • ~2/3 have prior violent records, often sexual offenses against children → prioritize known databases.
AMBER Alert & CARD

AMBER Alert grew from the 1996 Amber Hagerman case; all 50 states have one. Griffin & Miller (2008) caution: "Slow response does not necessarily cause death — the offender's intent makes any response too slow." Gier et al. (2012) recommend posting both "clean" and "disheveled" photos to boost recognition.

CARD (Child Abduction Rapid Deployment) — FBI since 2006; of 26 deployments by late 2007, 42% recovered alive, 50% dead, 8% still missing.

2 · 連續謀殺 — FBI 2005 研討會新共識

2005 年 San Antonio 的 FBI BAU-2 多學科研討會（135 位專家、10 國）重新定義：

Serial Murder
  • ● 同一兇手
  • ● ≥ 2 名受害者
  • ● 不同事件、不同時間
  • ● 中間有「冷靜期」
Mass Murder
  • ● ≥ 3 名
  • ● 同一地點
  • ● 無冷靜期

研討會建議廢除「spree murder」標籤 — 與 mass murder 難以區分。

研討會其他關鍵共識

  • 連續殺人犯沒有單一通用模板；生物、社會、心理因素的「罕見匯流」。
  • 大多並非嚴重精神疾患；有些具典型 psychopath 特徵（衝動、控制慾、無悔意、掠奪）。
  • 受害者選擇三要素：Availability + Vulnerability + Desirability。
  • 性動機型多在成長階段「erotic 化暴力」 — 暴力與性快感緊密交織。

受害者與被害學

Godwin (2000)：90% 受害者為陌生人。常選妓女、街頭流浪者、農工 — 「易失蹤而不引起警鈴」。Jenkins (1993) 提醒：我們對連續殺人犯的「知識」其實由社會對「哪些受害者值得查」決定。Calvin Jackson 1974 案沒人察覺他是連續殺人犯 — 因受害者都是被遺忘的窮老年人。

2 · Serial Murder — FBI 2005 Consensus

The 2005 San Antonio multidisciplinary FBI BAU-2 symposium (135 experts, 10 countries) redefined:

Serial Murder
  • ● Same offender
  • ● ≥ 2 victims
  • ● Separate events, different times
  • ● "Cooling-off period" between
Mass Murder
  • ● ≥ 3 victims
  • ● One location
  • ● No cooling-off

The symposium recommended dropping "spree murder" as a label — indistinguishable from mass.

Other Key Consensus

  • No single template — biological, social, psychological factors in a "rare confluence."
  • Most do not have major mental disorders; some have classic psychopath traits (impulsivity, control, no remorse, predation).
  • Victim selection triad: Availability + Vulnerability + Desirability.
  • Sexually motivated killers often "eroticized violence" during development.

Victims & Victimology

Godwin (2000): 90% of victims are strangers. Common targets: prostitutes, runaways, farm workers — "can disappear without alarm." Jenkins (1993) reminds us: what we "know" about serial killers is filtered by which victims police take seriously. Calvin Jackson (1974) was undetected — his victims were forgotten poor elders.

3 · 殺手分類兩派 — Holmes-DeBurger vs Canter

3.1 Holmes-DeBurger（動機導向）

V

Visionary 幻視型

精神病性、受幻覺驅使（God/demon mandated）；現場混亂、留下大量物證；無 IVT；對 GP 有利（多在 comfort zone）。

M

Mission-Oriented 使命型

自認須消滅某類「不潔」群體（妓女、特定宗教族群）；非精神病；現場為混合型 (偏向 disorganized)。

H

Hedonistic 享樂型

三亞型：

  • Lust：完全為性（含 necrophilia）
  • Thrill：享受受害者恐懼
  • Comfort：殺為奪取財產 / 生活改善（女性連續殺人犯多屬此 — 黑寡婦、死亡天使）
P

Power/Control 權力控制型

核心：對受害者擁有絕對的生死控制權；目標脆弱（如兒童）；現場高度組織化。

3.2 Canter & Youngs（角色主題導向）

Canter 認為 Holmes-DeBurger 過度倚賴 motive 推論，缺乏實證支持。他改以「兇手如何對待受害者」分三主題：

主題定義 / 行為線索
Object 物件完全去人化 — 截肢、斬首、多處重傷、無同理。
Vehicle 載體受害者是情緒攻擊的工具 — 帶有特定意義、虐待但未到 Object 程度。
Person 人扭曲的擬親密 — 咬痕（5% 命案）、安撫語言、表現出「認識你」。

一位兇手通常跨案一致採用同一主題 — 對 linkage 有幫助。

3 · Two Schools of Killer Typology

3.1 Holmes-DeBurger (Motive-Centered)

V

Visionary

Psychotic, driven by hallucinations (God/demon mandated); chaotic scene with abundant evidence; no IVT; favorable for GP (within comfort zone).

M

Mission-Oriented

Self-imposed duty to eliminate an "unworthy" group (prostitutes, certain religions); not psychotic; mixed (toward disorganized) scenes.

H

Hedonistic

Three subtypes:

  • Lust: purely sexual (necrophilia)
  • Thrill: relishes the victim's terror
  • Comfort: kills for material gain (most female serial killers — black widows, angels of death)
P

Power/Control

Absolute life-or-death control over the victim; targets the vulnerable (children); highly organized scenes.

3.2 Canter & Youngs (Role-Theme-Centered)

Canter argues Holmes-DeBurger overweights motive without evidence. Instead, classify by "how the offender treats the victim":

ThemeDefinition / Cues
ObjectFull dehumanization — dismemberment, decapitation, multiple severe injuries, no empathy.
VehicleVictim is a tool for emotional expression — meaningful, abused but not dehumanized.
PersonDistorted pseudo-intimacy — bite marks (5% of homicides), reassuring language, "I know you" cues.

A given killer consistently uses one theme — useful for linkage.

4 · 連續強姦 — 你以為的很少，實際的很多

4.1 被低估的數字

美國 2010 年通報強姦 ~84,767 件，但研究估計：

  • 64–96% 強姦未通報（Fisher 2000；Lisak 2010）
  • 通報的多數未起訴（特別是熟人強姦）
  • 大學男性 4% 是「未被偵測的連續強姦犯」（Lisak & Miller 2002）
  • 新進海軍中 13% 涉案，71% 為連續犯（McWhorter 等 2009）

Lisak & Miller 樣本中 4% 連續強姦犯造成 28% 的暴力 — 強姦並非「單次衝動」，而是「未偵破的連續犯」所為。

4.2 三大選擇 Scripts（Deslauriers-Varin & Beauregard 2010）

Script細分 Track典型情境
HomeIntrusion 闖入看準屋內女性獨自時破門
Invited 受邀先取得信任，受邀進門
OutdoorsNoncoercive逐次接近慢跑、讀書者，邀至他處

4.3 False Allegations

實證範圍 2–10%（Lisak 等 2010；Lonsway 2010）— 遠低於常見的「很多」誤解。Kanin (1994)：多數來自需要為合意性行為製造「不在場證明」。

4 · Serial Rape — Underestimated and Underprosecuted

4.1 The Hidden Numbers

~84,767 U.S. rapes reported in 2010, but research suggests:

  • 64–96% are never reported (Fisher 2000; Lisak 2010)
  • Most reported cases are not prosecuted (especially acquaintance rape)
  • ~4% of college men are undetected serial rapists (Lisak & Miller 2002)
  • New Navy enlistees: 13% offended, 71% serial (McWhorter et al. 2009)

4% of Lisak's sample produced 28% of the violence — rape is rarely a "one-off impulse"; it's mostly by undetected serial offenders.

4.2 Three Target-Selection Scripts (Deslauriers-Varin & Beauregard 2010)

ScriptTrackTypical scenario
HomeIntrusionBreaks in when woman is alone
InvitedGains trust, gets invited in
OutdoorsNoncoerciveGradually approaches a jogger/reader, lures to another place
CoerciveWaits outdoors, drags into seclusion (Teardrop Rapist)
SocialOnsiteAt bar/pool/mall/theatre — attacks in bathroom/changing room
Off-siteTakes victim from social setting elsewhere

4.3 False Allegations

Empirical range 2–10% (Lisak et al. 2010; Lonsway 2010) — far lower than the popular misperception. Kanin (1994): most arise from needing an "alibi" for a consensual encounter.

5 · Massachusetts Treatment Center 分類（MTC-R3）

MTC-R3 把陌生強姦犯分 4 主類、9 亞型。下方表格幫你快速定位：

主類核心必要行為線索
1–2 Opportunistic機會主義；非性幻想驅動對受害者冷漠、用力不超出必要、廣泛衝動性反社會史
3 Pervasively Angry長期高怒氣，對所有人暴力遠超出必要、無性興奮跡象、攻擊隨機未預謀
4–5 Sexually Motivated Sadistic性 + sadistic 幻想；overt 對外、muted 主要在幻想暴力與性興奮明確連結；目標含性意義部位
6–7 Sexually Motivated Non-Sadistic性興奮但低暴力；想「證明」自己的性能力自我安撫式語言、扭曲擬戀愛、關心受害者「感受」

每主類再依「社會能力」分高/低，得到 9 亞型。

6 · Canter 的「四主題」強姦分類

Canter 認為 MTC 只看內在動機不夠用 — 提出「兇手對待受害者的方式」四主題，更實用於現場側寫：

主題對應角色線索
Control 控制Object綁、矇眼、噤聲；武器；無敵意亦無同理
Hostility 敵意Vehicle言語暴力、辱罵、撕衣；整體攻擊性高
Involvement 涉入Person親吻、稱讚、期待受害者回應；扭曲擬親密
Theft 偷竊—強姦發生在搶劫/竊盜/劫車過程中，工具性目的
📊 112 件英國陌生強姦案分析（Canter 等 2003）：
  • Involvement：32%（最大）
  • Hostility：26%
  • Control：10%
  • Theft：5%
  • 73% 可清楚歸類

挑戰「強姦純為權力與控制」的傳統定見：多數陌生強姦其實展現（扭曲的）擬親密接觸。

6 · Canter's Four Themes of Rape

Canter argues MTC's motive-only view is too thin. He proposes four themes by how the offender treats the victim — more usable for scene profiling:

ThemeRoleCues
ControlObjectBind, blindfold, gag; weapon; no hostility, no empathy
HostilityVehicleVerbal violence, insults, torn clothing; high aggression
InvolvementPersonKisses, compliments, expects responses; distorted pseudo-intimacy
Theft—Rape during robbery/burglary/carjacking; instrumental
📊 112 UK Stranger Rapes (Canter et al. 2003):
  • Involvement: 32% (largest)
  • Hostility: 26%
  • Control: 10%
  • Theft: 5%
  • 73% classifiable with a clear dominant theme

Challenges the "rape = power/control" framing: most stranger rapes show distorted pseudo-intimacy.

7 · 連續縱火

7.1 數字與基本輪廓

  • 2010 美國 ~60,000 起縱火，~8,806 人被捕；40% 未滿 18 歲、23% 未滿 15 歲。
  • 連續縱火 = 3 件以上的有意縱火。
  • 對社區造成龐大財損與生命損失；多數連續縱火犯與其他犯罪重疊，部分有精神疾患。

7.2 兩個極端群組

🚒 消防員 / 調查員縱火犯

少數比例；多為入職 <3 年；動機是「想當英雄」；先從垃圾桶、草地等小火開始；常第一個到場。John L. Orr（Focus 5.3）即為極端例 — 加州縱火調查員兼連續縱火犯，1980s–90s 多起命案 4 人死亡，留下自己寫的小說《Points of Origin》。

👶 少年縱火犯

Icove & Estepp (1987)：96% 破壞性縱火為少年所為，多在自家 1 英里內；常為反抗權威、博同儕注目、單純興奮。家庭功能不彰、被虐待、學業困難、衝動 + 敵意都是預測因子。50% 留在現場觀看。

7.3 Canter-Fritzon (1998) 四主題模型

🔥 兩維度 × 兩極：
Expressive 表達性Instrumental 工具性
對 Person情緒困擾、被忽視；以縱火求關注失敗關係、對特定人的報復
對 Object連續縱火主要落點；象徵性對象（學校、教堂）；多數選無人建物少年破壞、商業詐欺、掩飾其他犯罪

7.4 Action System Model（Fritzon, Canter, Wilton 2001）

把 Canter-Fritzon 擴展為四主題：Expressive、Integrative、Conservative、Adaptive。
Integrative：精神疾患、自殺企圖、自焚都可能；
Conservative：以縱火影響特定他人（失敗關係復仇）；
Adaptive：用縱火掩蓋其他犯罪。

7.5 Pyromania

DSM 概念：多次有意縱火，無明顯動機，前期亢奮、後期釋放。實證支持稀薄，若存在亦極罕見。絕大多數連續縱火犯不符合「無法抗拒衝動」標準 — 對 profiling 實用價值有限。

7 · Serial Arson

7.1 Numbers and Baseline

  • 2010 U.S.: ~60,000 arsons; ~8,806 arrests; 40% under 18, 23% under 15.
  • Serial arson = ≥3 intentional fires.
  • Heavy community costs in money and lives; many serial arsonists overlap with other crime and some have mental disorders.

7.2 Two Extreme Groups

🚒 Firefighter / Investigator Arsonists

Small share; usually less than 3 years on the job; motive is "wanting to look heroic"; starts with nuisance fires (trash, brush); often first on scene. John L. Orr (Focus 5.3) — an LA-area fire investigator and serial arsonist behind 1980s–90s California fires (4 dead), and even wrote a novel, "Points of Origin."

👶 Juvenile Firesetters

Icove & Estepp (1987): 96% of vandalism arsons by juveniles, mostly within 1 mile of home; motives are defiance of authority, peer status, excitement. Predictors: family dysfunction, abuse, school trouble, impulsivity + hostility. 50% stay to watch.

7.3 Canter-Fritzon (1998) Four-Theme Model

🔥 Two dimensions × two poles:
ExpressiveInstrumental
Target = PersonEmotional distress, neglect; setting fires to draw attentionFailed relationships; revenge against a specific person
Target = ObjectMain locus of serial arson; symbolic targets (schools, churches); mostly uninhabited buildingsJuvenile vandalism, insurance fraud, cover for other crime

7.4 Action System Model (Fritzon, Canter & Wilton 2001)

Four themes: Expressive, Integrative, Conservative, Adaptive.
Integrative: mental disorder, suicide attempts, self-immolation possible;
Conservative: affecting a specific person (revenge after a failed relationship);
Adaptive: covering up other crime.

7.5 Pyromania

DSM concept: repeated intentional firesetting with no apparent motive, pre-arousal then post-relief. Empirical support is thin; if it exists, very rare. Most serial arsonists do NOT meet "irresistible impulse" — limited profiling value.

8 · 連續竊盜

竊盜常被視為「無聊的非暴力犯罪」，但 Canter 提醒：許多連續強姦犯的前科是竊盜，且住宅竊盜本質上具有人際侵入性。Merry & Harsent (2000) 四主題：

Implicit interpersonal 低Explicit interpersonal 高
Low craft 低技Pilferer 小竊賊 — 隨意拿易攜帶物品、少破壞、典型少年犯Intruder 闖入者 — 故意破壞、可能與住戶遭遇、衝動型
High craft 高技Raider 突襲者 — 專業、目標筆電/珠寶/古董、避免接觸Invader 入侵者 — 兼具工具性與表達性，破壞家庭照、塗鴉、留簽名

近端重複現象 (Near-Repeat)

Bowers & Johnson (2005)、Bernasco (2008)：

  • 初次竊案後 1 週內，鄰近房屋風險顯著升高；同一兇手所為。
  • 多在同一時段（兇手熟悉作息）。
  • 原因：兇手已建立 mental map；初次案件把房屋從「可能合適」變成「已知合適」；初次未帶走的可能再回來拿；新換的物品也可成新目標。

Bennell & Jones (2005)：「案件間的距離」與「案件間的時間」是住宅竊盜 linkage 最有效的兩個特徵 — 比 MO 更穩定。

8 · Serial Burglary

Burglary is often dismissed as "boring nonviolent crime," but Canter reminds us many serial rapists have burglary priors, and residential burglary is inherently interpersonal. Merry & Harsent (2000) four themes:

Low Interpersonal (implicit)High Interpersonal (explicit)
Low craftPilferer — grabs easy items, minimal damage; typically juvenileIntruder — deliberate damage, may meet occupants; impulsive
High craftRaider — professional, targets laptops/jewelry/antiques, avoids contactInvader — instrumental + expressive; destroys family photos, leaves messages

The Near-Repeat Phenomenon

Bowers & Johnson (2005), Bernasco (2008):

  • Within ~1 week of an initial burglary, nearby homes are at significantly higher risk; usually the same offender.
  • Often at the same time of day (offender knows schedules).
  • Why: mental map established; initial crime turns "presumed suitable" into "known suitable"; offender may return for items previously left behind or new replacements.

Bennell & Jones (2005): distance between crimes + time between crimes are the most consistent linkage features in residential burglary — more stable than MO.

10 · 本章總結

  1. 媒體式「典型綁架」極為罕見；3 小時是真正的關鍵窗口；兒童綁架兇手 ~67% MO 一致，多有暴力前科。
  2. FBI 2005 共識重新定義 serial murder（≥2 + 冷靜期）與 mass murder；建議廢除 spree murder。
  3. Holmes-DeBurger 動機型分類提供入門框架；但 Canter 的角色主題（Object/Vehicle/Person）更貼合現場可觀察線索。
  4. 強姦的「未偵測連續犯」遠多於通報案件；MTC-R3 + Canter 四主題（含 Involvement 最大）共同提供現代強姦側寫骨幹。
  5. Canter-Fritzon 四主題 + Action System Model 把縱火置於「表達／工具 × 對人／對物」的座標。
  6. Merry-Harsent 四主題（Pilferer/Raider/Intruder/Invader）+ near-repeat 給竊盜偵查地理與行為雙重支點。
  7. 所有 typology 都應作為「縮小範圍」工具，而非「最終結論」 — 個別差異仍勝過分類。
🎯 一句話帶走：「典型化讓我們快速理解，但只有例外能教會我們真實。」

10 · Chapter Summary

  1. Media-style "stereotypical abductions" are rare; the real window is 3 hours; child abductor-killers show ~67% MO consistency and usually have violent priors.
  2. The FBI 2005 consensus redefines serial murder (≥2 + cooling-off) and mass murder; spree murder dropped.
  3. Holmes-DeBurger motive types are a starting frame; Canter's role themes (Object/Vehicle/Person) map better to observable cues.
  4. "Undetected serial rapists" vastly outnumber reported cases; MTC-R3 + Canter's four themes (Involvement is largest) form the modern rape-profiling backbone.
  5. Canter-Fritzon + the Action System Model place arson on an "expressive/instrumental × person/object" grid.
  6. Merry-Harsent (Pilferer/Raider/Intruder/Invader) + near-repeat give burglary investigations both behavioral and geographic anchors.
  7. All typologies are narrowing tools, not conclusions — individual variation still beats categories.
🎯 One-liner takeaway: "Typologies help us grasp the patterns — but only the exceptions teach us the truth."
