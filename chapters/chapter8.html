1 · USS Iowa 案 — 心理解剖史上的轉折

1989 年 4 月 19 日，USS Iowa 戰艦在波多黎各外海進行例行射擊演習時，第二砲塔中央砲管接連三次猛烈爆炸 — 47 名海軍人員死亡。海軍 JAG 召開調查，並請 FBI NCAVC 執行 EDA。

FBI 的 EDA 結論

FBI 鎖定 Gunner's Mate Clayton Hartwig。理由：

  • 曾實驗爆裂物與引爆器
  • 對船難有「迷戀」
  • 高中時曾自殺未遂、爆炸前數週曾談到自殺
  • 「曾說『偏好以爆炸方式死亡』」
  • 近期與密友決裂

FBI 結論：「Hartwig 故意引爆，自殺並帶走 46 名同袍。」海軍司令據此聲明同意。

但接下來發生了什麼？

  1. APA 12 位專家受眾議院 HASC 委託獨立評估 → 10 位認為 FBI 分析無效。
  2. GAO + Sandia 國家實驗室技術調查 → 爆炸最可能是「過度推桿（over-ramming）」造成。火藥袋被以高速推進 42 英寸（比正常多 21 英寸），擠壓 2,700 磅彈頭 → 引爆。
  3. 無證據顯示 Hartwig 引發爆炸；但海軍拒絕重啟心理面調查。
⚠️ 教訓（Poythress 等 1993）：FBI 探員以「絕對確定」呈現結論，而非機率；他們完全未自行訪談，僅根據 NIS 提供的資訊；國會質疑 EDA 的科學基礎。本案使心理解剖第一次被廣泛檢視。

1 · The USS Iowa Case — A Turning Point for PA

On 19 April 1989, during a routine firing exercise off Puerto Rico, USS Iowa's Turret II center gun erupted in three rapid explosions — 47 naval personnel killed. The Navy JAG convened an investigation and requested an EDA from the FBI NCAVC.

FBI's EDA Conclusion

FBI focused on Gunner's Mate Clayton Hartwig:

  • Had experimented with explosives and detonators
  • "Fascination" with ship disasters
  • Attempted suicide in high school; spoke of suicide weeks before
  • "Said he preferred to die by explosion"
  • Recent falling out with a close friend

FBI: "Hartwig deliberately caused the explosion, taking 46 personnel with him." The CNO concurred.

What Happened Next?

  1. An APA panel of 12 experts commissioned by the U.S. House HASC reviewed → 10 found the FBI analysis invalid.
  2. GAO + Sandia National Laboratories technical investigation → most likely cause was over-ramming. Powder was driven 42 inches (21 inches past normal) against the 2,700-lb projectile → detonation.
  3. No evidence Hartwig caused the blast; yet the Navy refused to reopen the psychological side.
⚠️ Lessons (Poythress et al. 1993): FBI agents stated conclusions with absolute certainty rather than probability; they conducted no interviews of their own, relying only on NIS materials; Congress questioned EDA's scientific basis. This case was PA's first wide public scrutiny.

2 · 什麼是心理解剖？

「Psychological autopsy（PA）」也稱 equivocal death analysis (EDA) 或 reconstructive psychological evaluation。針對「已死亡個體」進行 — 透過遺留證據重建其死前生活、認知歷程、近期情緒與行為，藉此回答「死亡模式」是什麼。

歷史脈絡

1943

OSS 為 Hitler 寫的心理側寫（Langer）

本質上是對「已知個體」的心理分析，可視為 PA 的廣義雛形。

1958

Shneidman 與 L.A. 醫檢官 Theodore Murphy

面對大量「不明死亡」 → Shneidman 正式發明「psychological autopsy」一詞。

1987

Bruce Ebert 第一套執行指引

1989

USS Iowa 案 → 1990s

HASC 國會調查、Poythress et al. (1993) 系統批判 → PA 科學化辯論。

關鍵概念：Cause vs Mode of Death

🩺 Cause of Death

直接觸發死亡的生理機制 — 槍傷、缺氧、心肌梗塞、外傷。由醫檢官／病理學家判定。

🧠 Mode / Manner of Death

五類之一：自殺 / 他殺 / 意外 / 自然 / 未明。由 PA 試圖釐清。約 5–20% 的死亡為 equivocal。

Young (1992) 經典案例：跳傘者從 5,000 英尺墜地致死 — Cause 很清楚（多處重傷），但是意外、自殺（自行破壞傘）、他殺（被他人破壞傘）、還是自然（跳傘途中心肌梗塞）？這就是 PA 介入的地方。

2 · What Is Psychological Autopsy?

"Psychological autopsy (PA)" — also called equivocal death analysis (EDA) or reconstructive psychological evaluation. It targets a deceased individual, reconstructing their pre-death life, cognition, emotion, and behavior to determine the mode of death.

Historical Lineage

1943

OSS Hitler profile (Langer)

Essentially a psychological analysis of a known individual — a broad PA precursor.

1958

Shneidman with L.A. ME Theodore Murphy

Facing many unexplained deaths → Shneidman coined "psychological autopsy."

1987

Bruce Ebert's first guidelines

1989

USS Iowa → 1990s

HASC inquiry + Poythress et al. (1993) → PA scientific debate.

Key Concept: Cause vs Mode of Death

🩺 Cause of Death

The physiological mechanism directly triggering death — gunshot, hypoxia, MI, trauma. The medical examiner / pathologist's call.

🧠 Mode / Manner of Death

One of five: suicide / homicide / accident / natural / undetermined. PA tries to clarify. About 5–20% of deaths are equivocal.

Young (1992) classic example: a skydiver falls 5,000 feet to death. Cause: massive trauma. But — accident, suicide (self-rigged failure), homicide (someone else tampered), or natural (MI mid-jump)? That's where PA enters.

3 · 兩大類 PA + 三大用途

La Fon (2008) 兩大類

🪦 SPA — Suicide PA

當「已知是自殺」時，目的是了解促成自殺的心理社會因素 — 為預防、家屬療癒、診療反思。

❓ EDPA — Equivocal Death PA

當死亡模式不明時，目的是釐清是自殺、他殺、意外、自然或未明。

Ogloff & Otto (1993) 三大用途

R

研究（Research）

了解自殺的心理社會因素，改善預防與治療。

C

臨床實務（Clinical）

協助家屬走出陰影；機構檢討治療程序是否預防或可能促成自殺。

L

法律訴訟（Litigation）

多用於民事：

  • 保險（多數保單不賠自殺）
  • 遺囑能力（簽署時心智狀態）
  • 員工補償（工作壓力導致自殺）
  • 產品責任、醫療過失
  • 少數刑事案件（自殺 vs 偽裝他殺）
FOCUS 8.1

Vincent Foster 案（1993）

柯林頓政府的白宮高階律師 Foster 被發現死於 Fort Marcy Park，手持槍枝、頭部中彈。FBI 與美國國家公園警察判定自殺。但因正值 Whitewater 調查期，陰謀論不斷 — 三次獨立調查（Fiske、Starr 3 年研究、國會）皆確認自殺。

Berman 醫師執行 PA 後指出：Foster 死前 4 天向家友表達極度沮喪（工作量、媒體攻擊、Wall Street Journal 評論）；未留遺書 — 「多數自殺者『不』留遺書」。

3 · Two Types + Three Purposes

La Fon (2008): Two Types

🪦 SPA — Suicide PA

When suicide is known. Goal: understand psychosocial factors — for prevention, family healing, treatment reflection.

❓ EDPA — Equivocal Death PA

When the mode is unclear. Goal: classify as suicide, homicide, accident, natural, or undetermined.

Ogloff & Otto (1993): Three Purposes

R

Research

Understand suicide psychosocial factors; improve prevention and treatment.

C

Clinical

Help survivors heal; review whether treatment helped or contributed to suicide.

L

Litigation

Mostly civil:

  • Insurance (most policies don't cover suicide)
  • Testamentary capacity
  • Workers' comp (work stress → suicide)
  • Product liability, malpractice
  • Some criminal (suicide vs staged homicide)
FOCUS 8.1

Vincent Foster Case (1993)

Clinton White House lawyer Foster found dead at Fort Marcy Park, gun in hand, head wound. FBI + U.S. Park Police ruled suicide. Whitewater context fueled conspiracy theories — three independent investigations (Fiske, Starr's 3-year inquiry, Congress) all confirmed suicide.

Dr. Berman's PA: Foster expressed deep depression 4 days before death (workload, media attacks, WSJ editorials); left no note — "most suicides do NOT leave notes."

4 · 美國軍方 — PA 最大用戶

各軍種（陸海空海陸隊）對「基地內或軍人發生的所有 equivocal death」都須執行 EDPA。歷史變化：

2001 以前

陸軍每件自殺都需 PA — 蒐集資訊預防未來自殺。

2001 以後

僅 equivocal deaths 需 PA；執行者必須完成現場偵查 + 物理解剖 + 毒理 + 自殺行為訓練。

2008 DoDSER（DoD Suicide Event Report）

跨軍種統一通報系統。最近報告（2010）顯示軍中自殺主要壓力源：

  • 失敗親密關係：51%
  • 非親密關係失敗：14%
  • Article 15 非司法懲處：15%
  • 民事訴訟：13%
  • 已被診斷有精神疾患：36%
  • PTSD 為貢獻因素：僅 4%

伊拉克 / 阿富汗戰爭期間軍中自殺率達歷史新高。

4 · The Military — PA's Biggest Customer

Each branch (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps) must perform EDPA for every equivocal death on base or among personnel. Historical shift:

Pre-2001

Army required PA on every suicide — to inform prevention.

Post-2001

PA required only for equivocal deaths; conductors must be trained in crime scene + physical autopsy + toxicology + suicidal behavior.

2008 DoDSER (DoD Suicide Event Report)

Cross-service unified reporting. Latest report (2010) shows top military suicide stressors:

  • Failed intimate relationships: 51%
  • Other failed relationships: 14%
  • Article 15 non-judicial punishment: 15%
  • Civil legal problems: 13%
  • Diagnosed mental disorder: 36%
  • PTSD as contributing factor: only 4%

Suicide rates during Iraq/Afghanistan wars reached historic highs.

5 · Suicide by Cop（SbC）

Suicide by Cop（警察輔助自殺）

「個人故意以威脅生命之犯罪行為逼迫警員射殺自己以完成自殺」（Geberth 1993；Monahan 2001）。

占 美國警察致命射擊 10–13%（部分研究高達 25%）。

Hutson 等 (1998) LA 縣 10 年資料 — SbC 認定四要件

  1. 有當事人自殺意圖證據
  2. 當事人明確想被警員射殺
  3. 當事人持有致命武器（或看似致命的武器）
  4. 當事人故意升高衝突挑釁射擊

研究發現：

  • SbC 占 LA 縣 11% 警員致命射擊（1987–1997）
  • 受害者 98% 為男性；年齡 18–54 歲
  • 近 50% 持真槍、17% 持仿真槍
  • 持槍者被射殺機率 93%、持刀僅一部分
  • 多數有精神疾患或酒精／毒品影響
  • 許多人因宗教信仰、怕「失手」等原因無法自我了結
  • 多數無犯罪前科；有前科者多為家暴或藥物相關
📜 法律分類爭議：所有 SbC 案件在驗屍報告上均被列為「他殺」（因為由他人致命）— 但「自殺由警員執行」這個矛盾分類在醫檢官界仍有爭議（Neitzel & Gill 2011）。

5 · Suicide by Cop (SbC)

Suicide by Cop

"Individuals bent on self-destruction engage in life-threatening, criminal behavior to force police to kill them." (Geberth 1993; Monahan 2001)

Accounts for 10–13% of U.S. police lethal force cases (some studies up to 25%).

Hutson et al. (1998), 10-year LA County — Four SbC Criteria

  1. Evidence of suicidal intent
  2. Specifically wants officers to shoot
  3. Possesses a lethal (or apparently lethal) weapon
  4. Intentionally escalates and provokes shooting

Findings:

  • SbC = 11% of LA officer shootings (1987–1997)
  • 98% male, age 18–54
  • ~50% had real firearms; 17% replicas
  • 93% with guns were fatally shot; lower rates for knives
  • Many had mental disorders or alcohol/drug influence
  • Many couldn't bring themselves to self-kill due to religion or fear of "botching" it
  • Most had no record; some had domestic violence or drug priors
📜 Legal classification debate: All SbC cases are coded as "homicide" on death certificates (death by another) — but the "suicide carried out by an officer" paradox remains contested among medical examiners (Neitzel & Gill 2011).

6 · Autoerotic Asphyxia + 窒息遊戲

Autoerotic Asphyxia (AEA)：自慰時透過缺氧（hypoxia）尋求性興奮 — 因為腦缺氧的快感據信能增強性高潮強度。

Typical vs Atypical

Typical AEA

頸部束縛缺氧（皮帶、繩索、犬項圈、彈跳繩）。最常見也最易致死。「release mechanism（釋放裝置）」是 PA 判斷意外的重要線索（如快拆裝置、活結、腳能著地）。

Atypical AEA

不用頸部束縛 — 改用塑膠袋、面罩、丁烷或一氧化二氮、電擊、胸部或腹部加壓、潛水。

數據與線索

  • 北美每年估計 250–1,200 件 AEA 死亡 — 但無集中資料庫，可能嚴重低估。
  • 典型受害者：白人、年輕、中產、未婚男性。
  • 30% 男性受害者裸體被發現；25% 穿女性服裝；常有色情品、性玩具。
  • 男女比約 21:7（Sauvageau & Racette 2006）。
  • 家屬發現後常會「整理現場」 — 讓 PA 困難。

The Choking Game — 與 AEA 不同

主要為青少年追求短暫腦缺氧的快感（並非性目的）。別名：black hole、suffocation roulette、space monkey、California high…

CDC 報告：1995–2007 美國至少 82 件 6–19 歲青少年死亡。Macnab 等 (2009) 對美/加 2,504 名中學生：

  • 68% 聽過 / 45% 認識有人玩過 / 6.6% 自己玩過
  • 33% 兒科 / 家醫對此完全不熟悉（McClave 等 2010）
  • YouTube 上大量示範影片，常用「sleeper hold」（同伴從背後勒頸）

6 · Autoerotic Asphyxia + The Choking Game

Autoerotic Asphyxia (AEA): seeking sexual arousal through hypoxia during masturbation — the cerebral hypoxia euphoria reportedly intensifies orgasm.

Typical vs Atypical

Typical AEA

Neck ligature (belt, rope, dog collar, bungee). The most common and most deadly. A release mechanism (quick-release, slipknot, feet touching floor) is the PA's key cue for accidental rather than intentional death.

Atypical AEA

No neck ligature — plastic bags, masks, butane or nitrous oxide, electricity, chest/abdomen compression, submersion.

Numbers & Cues

  • North America: estimated 250–1,200 AEA deaths/year — no central database, likely under-reported.
  • Typical victim: white, young, middle-class, unmarried male.
  • 30% of male victims found nude; 25% in women's clothing; pornography and sex aids often present.
  • Male:female ratio ~21:7 (Sauvageau & Racette 2006).
  • Families often "tidy" the scene — complicating PA.

The Choking Game — Different from AEA

Adolescents seeking brief hypoxic euphoria — not sexual. Aliases: black hole, suffocation roulette, space monkey, California high…

CDC: at least 82 U.S. deaths (6–19) 1995–2007. Macnab et al. (2009) survey of 2,504 U.S./Canada middle/high schoolers:

  • 68% had heard / 45% knew a player / 6.6% had tried
  • 33% of pediatricians/family physicians totally unaware (McClave et al. 2010)
  • YouTube has many demos using the "sleeper hold" (peer chokes from behind)

7 · 如何執行心理解剖？

7.1 資料來源（多管齊下）

  • 人際訪談：近親、密友、同事、雇主、教師、醫療提供者
  • 檔案紀錄：醫療、心理治療、教育、軍方、就業；藥單列表
  • 個人書寫與數位足跡：信件、日記、email、Facebook 貼文、瀏覽紀錄
  • 法務與行政紀錄：社福、警方紀錄、財務紀錄、信用紀錄
  • 現場與居所：法醫報告、警方報告、毒理報告、實地踏勘

7.2 Gosling 等 (2002) 的居所符號學

「人們在自己的空間中活動，並用物品與符號表達自我」 — PA 必須親訪死者居所：

類型定義例子
Identity claims「這就是我」的自我宣告獎牌、海報、文憑、旅行紀念品
Feeling regulators調節情緒或環境氛圍的物品家庭照、紀念物、音樂、書籍
Behavioral residue日常行為無意間留下的物理痕跡收據、垃圾、酒漬、整潔／凌亂度

7.3 關鍵時間窗

死亡前 24/48/72 小時的行為特別重要：

  • 付清保單
  • 贈送重要物品
  • 安排家人或寵物的照顧
  • 突然向重要他人告別
  • 對「絕望」「無法承受」等字詞的表達

7.4 U.S. Army Suicide 三度分類

級別定義
First-degree蓄意、有計畫、預謀
Second-degree衝動、無計畫、在強烈挑釁或情境壓力下
Third-degree把自己置於風險但意圖較低；方法不致命、有救援設計；「不幸」死亡
非典型：精神病性／酒藥能力受損
非典型：Subintentioned death長期高風險、自我忽略、酒藥濫用 — 死者在自身死亡中扮演半意識角色

7.5 時效要求

盡量在死後「數週內」完成訪談；超過 6 個月記憶準確度大幅下降。同時須具備同理心、知情同意、保密原則（Murthy 2010）。

7 · How PA Is Performed

7.1 Multi-Source Inputs

  • Interviews: close family, intimates, coworkers, supervisors, teachers, healthcare providers
  • Archival records: medical, mental health, education, military, employment; pharmacy printouts
  • Personal writings & digital footprint: letters, diaries, emails, Facebook, browsing history
  • Legal & administrative records: social services, police, financial, credit
  • Scene & residence: coroner report, police report, toxicology, on-site visit

7.2 Gosling et al. (2002): Residential Semiotics

"People decorate their spaces with objects expressing self" — PA must visit the home:

TypeDefinitionExamples
Identity claims"This is who I am" declarationsAwards, posters, degrees, travel mementos
Feeling regulatorsItems regulating mood or atmosphereFamily photos, keepsakes, music, books
Behavioral residuePhysical traces of daily routinesReceipts, trash, wine stains, neatness/messiness

7.3 Critical Time Windows

The 24/48/72 hours before death are especially significant:

  • Paying off insurance
  • Giving away important possessions
  • Arranging care for family or pets
  • Suddenly farewelling significant others
  • Expressions of hopelessness or being "unable to bear it"

7.4 U.S. Army's Three-Degree Suicide Classification

DegreeDefinition
FirstDeliberate, planned, premeditated
SecondImpulsive, unplanned, under intense provocation
ThirdSelf-jeopardy with low intent; less lethal means, rescue provisions; "unlucky" death
Atypical: psychotic / intoxicatedImpaired capacity
Atypical: subintentioned deathChronic risk-taking, self-neglect, substance abuse — partial unconscious role

7.5 Time Requirement

Complete interviews within weeks; over 6 months, memory accuracy drops sharply. Empathy, informed consent, confidentiality required (Murthy 2010).

8 · 自殺遺書的調查價值

📊 重要事實：只有約 1/3 自殺者留下遺書（Haines, Williams & Lester 2011）。「無遺書」絕非「非自殺」的證據。

真實 vs 偽造遺書的研究

Jones & Bennell (2007)、Bennell 等 (2011) 發現可靠的區辨指標：

面向真實遺書偽造遺書
長度顯著較長較短
句子結構有許多短句、片段較完整
內容給倖存者具體指示（金錢、寵物、後事）缺乏具體指示
情感正面情感（love、dear、sweetheart）穿插絕望情感扁平或誇張
解釋對為何結束生命有解釋不解釋或太籠統

Bennell 等 (2011)：「正面情感穿插於絕望之中」—— 反映「認知決心 + 情緒矛盾」並存的狀態。

Canter (2005b) R. v. Guilfoyle 案

一名婦女被發現上吊；附近留有手寫遺書。原推定為自殺、不再深入調查。後來疑點浮現 — 丈夫是否強迫她寫下遺書並設局？

Canter 執行 PA 後認定：太太確實是自願結束生命。但法院「不」允許 Canter 的 PA 證據呈堂。丈夫被判謀殺，上訴法院維持原判：「PA 的學術現狀不允許其進入法庭」。

這一案件成為 PA 在英國司法上的關鍵先例 — Ch 9 將深入討論。

8 · The Investigative Value of Suicide Notes

📊 Key fact: Only about 1/3 of suicides leave notes (Haines, Williams & Lester 2011). "No note" is NOT evidence against suicide.

Genuine vs Fake Notes

Jones & Bennell (2007), Bennell et al. (2011): reliable distinguishing markers:

DimensionGenuineFake
LengthSignificantly longerShorter
Sentence structureMany short fragmentsMore complete
ContentSpecific instructions to survivors (money, pets, arrangements)Lacks specific instructions
AffectPositive affect (love, dear, sweetheart) interspersed with despairFlat or overblown
ExplanationWhy ending lifeNo or too-vague explanation

Bennell et al. (2011): "Positive affect interspersed throughout despair" — reflecting "cognitive resolution + emotional ambivalence."

Canter (2005b) — R. v. Guilfoyle

A woman found hanged; a handwritten suicide note nearby. Initially ruled suicide and not investigated further. Later doubt — did the husband force her to write it and set the scene?

Canter's PA: she did take her own life. But the court did NOT allow Canter's PA as evidence. The husband was convicted of murder; the appellate court upheld it: "the academic state of PA doesn't permit courtroom use."

A key UK precedent — covered further in Ch 9.

9 · PA 的信度、效度與標準化

9.1 Reliability 信度

Inter-rater Reliability

「不同心理／精神專業人員對同一案件做出相同模式分類（自殺／意外／他殺等）的程度。」USS Iowa 案中 12 位 APA 專家中 10 位反對 FBI 結論 — 顯示當時 PA 缺乏 inter-rater reliability。

9.2 Validity 效度

「PA 是否準確判定 equivocal death 是自殺、意外或他殺？」 — 這個問題比 reliability 更難回答，原因：

  • 死者已不在場 — 無法直接確認
  • 家屬、朋友因內疚、羞恥可能提供失真資訊
  • 媒體大幅報導下，近親可能擔心言論被洩漏而保留
  • 缺乏可比對的「黃金標準」

9.3 標準化的困境

Selkin (1994)：「明確、確定的程序仍未發展。」Shneidman (1976) 16 點清單是雛形，但欠 inter-rater 信度檢驗。沒有 PA 全國認證制度，不同實務者依各自訓練、經驗、敏感度而結論不同。

9.4 Poythress 等 (1993) 三大建議

  1. 不宜將 PA 程序擴展至「非死亡情境」（如竊盜、綁架），除非信效度確立。
  2. 在法律情境中，PA 不應對死者死亡當下確切心理狀態做出絕對結論；只應提供「informed speculations or theoretical formulations」。
  3. 實務者不應對 PA 結果的精準度誤導使用者；研究尚不支持絕對性結論。
⚠️ Ch 9 預告：PA 在 Daubert / Frye 等可採性標準下，是否能進入法庭？目前許多法院認為「PA 的學術現狀不允許其進入法庭」（如 R. v. Guilfoyle）。

9 · Reliability, Validity, & Standardization

9.1 Reliability

Inter-rater Reliability

"The degree to which different mental health professionals classify the same case into the same mode (suicide/accident/homicide/etc.)." In USS Iowa, 10 of 12 APA experts rejected the FBI's conclusion — showing low inter-rater reliability of PA at the time.

9.2 Validity

"Does PA accurately determine whether equivocal death was suicide, accident, or homicide?" — harder than reliability because:

  • The deceased cannot confirm
  • Family/friends may distort from guilt, shame
  • In media-saturated cases, intimates may hold back fearing leaks
  • No "gold standard" for comparison

9.3 Standardization Difficulties

Selkin (1994): "Clear, definitive procedures have not been developed." Shneidman's (1976) 16-point checklist is a start but lacks inter-rater tests. No national PA certification — different practitioners reach different conclusions by background.

9.4 Poythress et al. (1993): Three Recommendations

  1. Don't extend PA to non-death situations (burglary, kidnapping) until validated.
  2. In legal contexts, PA should NOT draw categorical conclusions about precise mental state — only "informed speculations or theoretical formulations."
  3. Practitioners shouldn't mislead consumers about PA accuracy; the research doesn't yet support absolute conclusions.
⚠️ Ch 9 Preview: Can PA pass Daubert / Frye admissibility tests? Many courts currently rule that "the academic state of PA doesn't permit courtroom use" (e.g., R. v. Guilfoyle).

11 · 本章總結

  1. 心理解剖（PA / EDA / 重建心理評估）是側寫的第五大類 — 對已死亡個體進行，判斷「mode of death」。
  2. USS Iowa 案展示了「絕對自信的 EDA」如何造成傷害；GAO 與 Sandia 推翻 FBI 結論，APA 12 專家中 10 位反對。
  3. Cause of death 由病理學家判定；Mode 由 PA 釐清；5–20% 死亡為 equivocal。
  4. SPA（已知自殺）與 EDPA（模式不明）兩大類；三大用途為研究、臨床、訴訟。
  5. 美國軍方是 PA 最大用戶；DoDSER 顯示親密關係失敗為最大壓力源。
  6. SbC 占美國警員致命射擊 10–13%；Hutson 等四要件。
  7. AEA（typical vs atypical）與青少年「窒息遊戲」是常被誤判的 PA 案例。
  8. PA 執行需多源資料 + Gosling 三類符號（identity claims / feeling regulators / behavioral residue）+ 死前 24/48/72 小時行為。
  9. 只有 1/3 自殺者留遺書；真實遺書較長、含具體指示與穿插正面情感。
  10. PA 的信度與效度仍未完全確立；標準化進行中；Poythress 三大建議強調謹慎。
🎯 一句話帶走：「死者無法替自己說話 — 心理解剖必須謙卑、結構化、留有可被推翻的空間。」

11 · Chapter Summary

  1. The psychological autopsy (PA / EDA / reconstructive psychological evaluation) is profiling's fifth area — performed on the deceased to determine the mode of death.
  2. USS Iowa showed how an overconfident EDA can devastate; GAO + Sandia refuted FBI's conclusion; 10 of 12 APA experts disagreed.
  3. Cause is the pathologist's call; Mode is what PA targets; 5–20% of deaths are equivocal.
  4. SPA (known suicide) and EDPA (unclear mode); three purposes: research, clinical, litigation.
  5. The U.S. military is PA's biggest user; DoDSER reveals failed intimate relationships as the top stressor.
  6. SbC accounts for 10–13% of U.S. police lethal force; Hutson et al.'s four criteria.
  7. AEA (typical vs atypical) and the adolescent "choking game" are often misclassified PA cases.
  8. PA inputs: multi-source data + Gosling's three categories (identity claims / feeling regulators / behavioral residue) + the 24/48/72-hour pre-death behavior.
  9. Only 1/3 of suicides leave notes; genuine notes are longer with specific instructions and positive affect interspersed.
  10. PA's reliability and validity aren't fully established; standardization is ongoing; Poythress's three recommendations emphasize caution.
🎯 One-liner takeaway: "The dead can't speak — psychological autopsy must be humble, structured, and falsifiable."
