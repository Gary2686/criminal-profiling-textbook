1 · 概觀：什麼是 profiling？

20 世紀末到 21 世紀初，「側寫師（profilers）」幾乎無所不在。電視談話節目、暢銷小說、影集（《沉默的羔羊》、《Criminal Minds》、《Cracker》、《Profiler》），都把側寫師塑造成「直覺敏銳、能讀懂兇手心思」的神秘角色。

但學術文獻提醒我們：profiler 的資格落差極大 — 有些是有多年經驗的執法人員或心理學家，有些只是「擁有可疑函授學位、想登上媒體版面」的人物。

📍 反例：Beltway Sniper（華盛頓狙擊手）案 (2002)

3 週內 10 死 3 傷，側寫師告訴警方兇手「應為白人、單獨犯案、住在附近」並開白色廂型車。實際逮捕的是兩名非裔美國人 — 41 歲的 John Allen Muhammad 與 17 歲的 Lee Boyd Malvo — 駕駛藍色 Chevrolet Caprice 轎車。錯誤側寫讓警方在十多次盤查中放過真兇。

Hollywood effect 與「沒有 Cracker」

Canter, Alison, Alison 與 Wentink (2004) 觀察到：profiling 的相關資訊「主要透過大眾書籍而非同行審查的學術期刊」傳播。當「粗糙又未驗證的理論」搬上電影與電視時，就形成所謂的 Hollywood effect（Canter & Youngs, 2003）。

"There is no Cracker."
— 一位接受研究訪談的偵探（Gekoski & Gray, 2011）

翻成白話：「真實世界裡，沒有電視劇裡那種完美無瑕的側寫師。」

1 · Overview: What Is Profiling?

By the late 20th and early 21st centuries, "profilers" had become ubiquitous. Talk shows, bestselling novels, and TV series (Silence of the Lambs, Criminal Minds, Cracker, Profiler) cast profilers as almost mystical figures with uncanny insight into the criminal mind.

But the scholarly literature reminds us: profilers' credentials vary enormously — some are seasoned law-enforcement veterans or trained psychologists; others hold dubious diplomas and chase media attention.

📍 Cautionary Case: Beltway Sniper (2002)

Over three weeks, 10 were killed and 3 critically wounded. Profilers told police the sniper was probably white, acted alone, and lived nearby — driving a white panel van. The actual culprits were two African Americans, 41-year-old John Allen Muhammad and 17-year-old Lee Boyd Malvo, driving a blue Chevrolet Caprice. Their car was stopped at least 10 times — and waved through every time.

The Hollywood Effect & "No Cracker"

Canter, Alison, Alison, and Wentink (2004) note that profiling information "is most often disseminated via popular books rather than peer-reviewed journals." When loosely formulated theories make it onto film and TV, the result is the Hollywood effect (Canter & Youngs, 2003).

"There is no Cracker."
— a detective interviewed in Gekoski & Gray (2011)

In plain English: the flawless, intuitive profiler of TV simply does not exist in real life.

2 · Early History of Profiling

Profiling has a documented history stretching back roughly 500 years. The timeline below walks through the key milestones:

1486

Malleus Maleficarum（《女巫之槌》）

兩名道明會修士奉天主教會之命，撰寫此書作為「辨識、審訊、根除」女巫的官方手冊。它指引獵巫者尋找身上有疤痕、痣或胎記、會吟唱咒語或使用草藥的人，並描述各種逼供方法（如以拇指吊掛、寒室裸體加上拇指夾）。28 個版本在 1486–1600 年間流傳整個中世紀歐洲。

意義：這是已知最早的「系統性側寫文件」 — 雖然其方法殘酷、毫無實證基礎。

Commissioned by the Catholic Church, this guidebook by two Dominican monks instructed witch-hunters to look for body marks (scars, moles, birthmarks), incantations, or herbal remedies — and prescribed torture methods (hanging by thumbs, naked confinement with thumbscrews). Twenty-eight editions circulated through medieval Europe between 1486–1600.

Significance: the earliest known systematic "profiling" document — brutal and unscientific, but historically pivotal.

1841

Edgar Allan Poe — C. Auguste Dupin

Poe 的短篇《莫爾格街兇殺案》塑造了文學上第一位「側寫師」：怪僻的法國警官 Dupin。他以「ratiocination（推理）」破案 — 結合理性思考與生動想像力「把自己放進兇手心裡」。Bourque 等人 (2009) 認為這是現代側寫的雛形。

Poe's short story The Murders in the Rue Morgue introduced the first fictional profiler: the eccentric Parisian Dupin. He solved crimes via "ratiocination" — combining rational thought with a vivid imagination to "place himself into the criminal's mind." Bourque et al. (2009) view this as the prototype of modern profiling.

1887

Sir Arthur Conan Doyle — Sherlock Holmes

福爾摩斯的「演繹推理」是側寫的另一種文化原型 — 雖然偏向法醫推論而非心理側寫。他建立了「從微小細節重建罪犯特徵」的敘事模式，深深影響後世的偵探小說。

Holmes's "deductive reasoning" became another cultural prototype — closer to forensic inference than psychological profiling, but it established the narrative template of reconstructing the offender from tiny clues that defined the detective genre.

1888

Jack the Ripper · Dr. George Baxter Phillips

倫敦東區發生 5 起妓女連續殺害案。首席法醫病理學家 Phillips 注意到受害者體內器官切除「乾淨而精準」，從而推論兇手對解剖學有相當了解（Turvey, 2012）。這被視為真實案件中最早的側寫嘗試之一。案件迄今未破。

Five prostitutes were murdered in London's East End. Chief forensic pathologist Phillips noted that internal organs had been removed with cleanliness and precision, inferring sophisticated anatomical knowledge (Turvey, 2012). This is widely seen as one of the first real-world profiling attempts. The case remains unsolved.

1940s

William Langer — 二戰時期希特勒側寫

美國戰略情報局（OSS）的情報員 Langer，從所有公開資料中整理出對 Adolf Hitler 的「人物側寫」，提供軍方決策參考。此類「已知個人」的側寫，正是第 6 章將討論的 psychological profiling 早期形式。

OSS intelligence officer William Langer assembled an extensive profile of Adolf Hitler from publicly available material to support military decision-making — an early form of the psychological profiling of known individuals (covered in Chapter 6).

💡 觀念連結：現代行為側寫綜合了「精神病學的人格分析」「犯罪學的統計研究」「心理學的認知與動機理論」與「執法經驗」 — 不再倚賴上述任何單一傳統。
💡 Concept Link: Modern behavioral profiling integrates psychiatric personality analysis, criminological statistics, psychological theories of cognition and motivation, and law-enforcement experience — no longer relying on any single tradition above.

3 · 瘋狂炸彈客案（George Metesky, 1940–1956）

這是美國史上第一個被警方公開徵詢「側寫」且廣泛被媒體報導的案件。它讓 Dr. James Brussel 一夕成名，也讓 profiling 走進公眾視野。

3.1 案件梗概

  • 16 年累計約 47 枚自製管狀炸彈，目標地點極為多樣 — Con Edison 大樓、大中央車站、Radio City 音樂廳、Penn Station、紐約公共圖書館、Macy's、地鐵站、電話亭、戲院、巴士站等。
  • 炸彈署名 "F. P." — 後來證實是 "fair play（公平對待）"。
  • 炸彈設計用銅管加火藥（疑為步槍彈藥）、配糖與乾電池作引信。
  • 多數炸彈未爆或未造成傷亡，但 1952 年 12 月 Lexington Theatre 首次造成人員受傷。
  • 警方曾盤查紐約都會區 9,750 名有精神病史的人，重點放在「機械相關技能」者（製模工、機械師、電工、水管工）。
  • 16 年期間，警方曾逮錯人一次，該嫌犯被送進 Bellevue 進行 37 天精神鑑定期間，又出現新炸彈，證實人錯了。

3.2 Brussel 的側寫（1956 年聖誕節《紐約時報》刊出）

1956 年 12 月，紐約警察局長下令展開「警局史上最大規模搜捕」。Dr. James A. Brussel — 紐約州心理衛生部副局長 — 是少數被公開的諮詢顧問。在審視所有資料數小時後，他口頭給出了下列側寫：

Brussel 原始側寫摘要Brussel's Profile (verbatim)
"Single man, between 40 and 50 years old, introvert. Unsocial but not anti-social. Skilled mechanic. Cunning. Neat with tools. Egotistical of mechanical skill. Contemptuous of other people. Resentful of criticism of his work but probably conceals resentment. Moral. Honest. [Not] interested in women. High school graduate. Expert in civil or military ordnance. Religious. Might flare up violent at work when criticized. Possible motive: discharge or reprimand. Feels superior to critics. Resentment keeps growing. Present or former Consolidated Edison worker. Probably case of progressive paranoia."
— Meagher, 1956

3 · The Mad Bomber Case (George Metesky, 1940–1956)

This was the first U.S. case in which police openly sought a "profile" from an outside expert — and it was massively publicized. It catapulted Dr. James Brussel to fame and put profiling into the public consciousness.

3.1 Case Summary

  • About 47 homemade pipe bombs over 16 years, planted at Con Edison HQ, Grand Central Terminal, Radio City Music Hall, Penn Station, the NY Public Library, Macy's, subway stations, phone booths, theatres, and bus terminals.
  • Bombs were signed "F. P." — later revealed to mean "fair play."
  • Brass-pipe-and-gunpowder devices (likely rifle powder) using a sugar-and-dry-cell triggering mechanism.
  • Most failed to detonate or caused no injury, but the Lexington Theatre bomb in December 1952 was the first to wound someone.
  • Police investigated 9,750 NYC-area residents with mental-illness histories, focusing on those with mechanical skills (toolmakers, machinists, electricians, plumbers).
  • Police even arrested the wrong man once; during his 37-day psychiatric evaluation, another bomb went off, proving they had the wrong suspect.

3.2 Brussel's Profile (NY Times, Christmas Day 1956)

In December 1956, the NYPD Commissioner ordered "the greatest manhunt in the department's history." Dr. James A. Brussel — Assistant Commissioner of NY State Mental Hygiene — was one of the few consultants publicly identified. After hours of reviewing the material, he dictated this profile:

Brussel's Profile (verbatim)
"Single man, between 40 and 50 years old, introvert. Unsocial but not anti-social. Skilled mechanic. Cunning. Neat with tools. Egotistical of mechanical skill. Contemptuous of other people. Resentful of criticism of his work but probably conceals resentment. Moral. Honest. [Not] interested in women. High school graduate. Expert in civil or military ordnance. Religious. Might flare up violent at work when criticized. Possible motive: discharge or reprimand. Feels superior to critics. Resentment keeps growing. Present or former Consolidated Edison worker. Probably case of progressive paranoia."
— Meagher, 1956

FOCUS 1.1

Metesky 與媒體

Metesky 在 16 年犯案期間，與《紐約論壇報》、《Journal American》與《紐約時報》維持書信往來。《Journal American》甚至承諾調查他控告 Con Ed 的賠償案。Metesky 後來承認自己幾乎要走進 Journal American 編輯室「找人聊聊」 — 「我覺得我必須找個人講話。」

媒體學者批評：報紙在這個過程中「常踩到倫理界線」，並讓自己被 Metesky 操弄。但 Journal American 在他被捕後當天即聘請知名律師代表他打賠償官司 — 履行了當初的承諾。

Metesky and the Media

Throughout his 16-year campaign, Metesky corresponded with the NY Herald Tribune, the Journal American, and the NY Times. The Journal American even promised to investigate his compensation claim against Con Ed. Metesky later confessed he almost walked into their newsroom for advice — "I felt I had to talk to someone."

Media scholars charge that the press "often skirted ethical boundaries" and let itself be manipulated. Yet within hours of Metesky's arrest, the paper hired a prominent attorney to pursue his compensation claim — keeping its promise.

3.4 案件是怎麼真正破的？

真正的英雄並不是 Brussel，而是 Alice G. Kelly — Con Edison 的檔案管理員（一說由公司指派、一說自願）。她被某些信件中的字句吸引：「injustices（不公）」「take justice in my own hands（親手伸張正義）」。

Kelly 從 Con Ed 中被標記為 troublesome 的舊員工檔案翻出 George P. Metesky 的紀錄 — 包含照片、住址、工作紀錄與類似措辭的舊信件。Kelly 在事後拒絕了懸賞金，認為「我只是在做我的工作」。

幾週後，1957 年初，53 歲的 Metesky 在康乃狄克 Waterbury 被捕。當警方進門時，他剛剛從床上起來，「你們不是來查意外的，我猜你們懷疑我是瘋狂炸彈客」。當警方要他換衣服時，他穿著一套扣好的雙排扣西裝出現。

⚖️ 案件法律後續：由於追訴時效，僅能起訴 1952 年 3 月後的罪行。Metesky 被判定不適合受審（incompetent to stand trial），送 Matteawan 州立醫院監護約 16 年。直到 1972 年 Jackson v. Indiana 案後，醫師判定他無危險性，1973 年 12 月釋放。他原本身體羸弱，被釋放後竟再活 20 年，享年 90 歲。

3.4 How Was the Case Actually Solved?

The real hero was not Brussel but Alice G. Kelly, a Con Edison filing clerk (accounts differ on whether she was assigned or volunteered). Phrases in the letters caught her eye: "injustices," "take justice in my own hands."

From Con Ed's files marked troublesome, she pulled the dusty record of George P. Metesky — complete with photos, address, work history, and earlier letters using similar language. Kelly later declined the reward — "I was just doing my job."

Weeks later, in early 1957, 53-year-old Metesky was arrested at his Waterbury, CT home. When officers entered his bedroom, he greeted them: "You're looking for more than an accident… I guess you suspect that I am the mad bomber." When told to dress, he emerged in a buttoned double-breasted suit.

⚖️ Legal Aftermath: Because of the statute of limitations, charges covered only crimes from March 1952 onward. Metesky was declared incompetent to stand trial and committed to Matteawan State Hospital for ~16 years. After Jackson v. Indiana (1972), doctors found him not dangerous, and he was released in December 1973. Once frail, he then lived another 20 years, dying at 90.

4 · Brussel Legacy 與後見之明偏誤

Brussel 之所以被稱為「側寫之父」，原因有二：

  1. Mad Bomber 案是 美國史上第一個被媒體大肆報導的「警方求助於側寫」案件。
  2. 1970 年代初，他被 FBI 行為科學組（BSU）主任 Howard Teten 邀請參與 FBI 探員訓練 — 直接影響 FBI 的側寫實務。

然而，現代學者（Foster, 2000; Gladwell, 2009; Ramsland, 2009）指出：他在 1968 年回憶錄《Casebook of a Crime Psychiatrist》中描寫的側寫，遠比 1956 年實際刊登在報紙上的內容更精準 — 這正是經典的後見之明偏誤。

什麼是 Hindsight Bias（後見之明偏誤）？

Hindsight Bias

「在獲得新資訊後，傾向把原本的判斷『修改成更接近新資訊』的方向。」 — Mazzoni & Vannucci (2007)

🧠 後見之明偏誤的流程：
原始判斷
「兇手可能是西斯人」
(模糊的猜測)
→
事件揭曉
真兇來自立陶宛
(獲得新資訊)
→
重新陳述
「我早就說過
他是斯拉夫人」
(回憶被改寫)

為什麼專家更容易陷入這種偏誤？ Musch & Wagner (2007) 指出：「想要保護自己的專業聲譽」會強化這種傾向。Ash (2009) 補充：當原始預測「不確定」或「根本搞錯」時，hindsight bias 最容易發生。

Brussel 回憶錄裡的「神奇預測」

  • 雙排扣西裝且扣好鈕扣 — 1956 年原始報導中 未提及。事實：1940–50 年代雙排扣西裝佔西裝銷量 50% 以上（Chenoune, 1993），中年男性面對媒體扣好西裝幾乎是當時的常識。
  • 斯拉夫裔、住 Westchester — 因為信件郵戳多來自 Westchester（為紐約郊區，斯拉夫人聚居）。實際上 Metesky 是立陶宛裔且住康乃狄克。
  • Freudian 詮釋：Brussel 認為 Metesky 寫的圓形 W 像「女性乳房」或「陰囊」，劃破戲院椅墊則是「想穿透母親、閹割父親」的潛意識表現。這些臆測完全無法被科學驗證。
⚠️ 大量預測策略：Gladwell (2009) 銳利地評論：「Brussel 並沒有真正理解兇手的心智。他理解的是 — 如果你做很多預測，錯的會被遺忘，對的會讓你成名。」這也是當代某些 profiler 私下承認的潛規則（Finn, 2008）。但這背後付出的代價是：多餘的錯誤可能讓警方走進「無謂的偵查路徑」(wild goose chase)。

4 · The Brussel Legacy & Hindsight Bias

Brussel earned the "father of profiling" title for two reasons:

  1. The Mad Bomber case was the first widely publicized U.S. case where police consulted a profiler.
  2. In the early 1970s, FBI Behavioral Science Unit director Howard Teten invited him to help train agents — directly shaping FBI profiling practice.

However, modern scholars (Foster, 2000; Gladwell, 2009; Ramsland, 2009) note that the profile described in Brussel's 1968 memoir Casebook of a Crime Psychiatrist is far more accurate than what was actually published in 1956 — a textbook case of hindsight bias.

What Is Hindsight Bias?

Hindsight Bias

"The tendency to change a previous judgment in the direction of newly provided information." — Mazzoni & Vannucci (2007)

🧠 The Hindsight Loop:
Initial Judgment
"Probably an Eastern European…"
(vague guess)
→
Reveal
Offender is Lithuanian
(new information)
→
Restated
"I knew he was a Slav
all along"
(memory rewritten)

Why are experts especially vulnerable? Musch & Wagner (2007) argue that "the motive to protect one's professional reputation" reinforces hindsight bias. Ash (2009) adds that it spikes when the original prediction was uncertain or simply wrong.

Brussel's "Magical" Memoir Predictions

  • Buttoned double-breasted suit — never mentioned in 1956 reports. Fact: over 50% of suits sold in the 1940s–50s were double-breasted (Chenoune, 1993); a middle-aged man buttoning up for the press was essentially the default look.
  • Slav, lives near Westchester — based on Westchester postmarks. In reality, Metesky was Lithuanian and lived in Waterbury, CT.
  • Freudian interpretations: Brussel claimed Metesky's rounded W's looked like "female breasts" or a "scrotum," and that slashing theatre seats symbolized "penetrating his mother and castrating his father." These speculations are beyond scientific scrutiny.
⚠️ Volume Strategy: Gladwell (2009) cuts deep: "Brussel did not really understand the mind of the Mad Bomber. He seems to have understood only that, if you make a great number of predictions, the ones that were wrong will soon be forgotten, and the ones that turn out to be true will make you famous." Some contemporary profilers privately admit the same (Finn, 2008). But the cost is real: extra wrong predictions can send investigators on a "wild goose chase."

Barnum Statements（巴納姆陳述句）

「過於籠統的詞句，可以套用到許多不同的人身上。」名字來自馬戲團大亨 P. T. Barnum 的一句廣告詞：「我們什麼都賣（We've got something for everyone）」。

換言之：給一張這樣的清單，幾乎任何人都會說「嗯，這描述就是我」。

6 · 波士頓勒殺者案（1962–1964）

6.1 案件概況

  • 1962 年 6 月 14 日 至 1964 年 1 月 4 日，波士頓地區 13 名單身女性被殺害；至少 11 名被認為是「波士頓勒殺者」所為。
  • 所有受害者都在自家公寓內遇害、遭性侵（有時被以物品侵犯）、屍體被擺成羞辱性姿勢。
  • 都以受害者自己的衣物（絲襪、皮帶）勒斃。
  • 沒有強行進入跡象，警方推測受害者可能認識兇手。
  • 前 5 名年齡 55–85 歲，之後出現 21 歲受害者，再來年齡分散。

6.2 Brussel 的 Freudian 詮釋

當時心理學界、犯罪學界、精神醫學界多數認為「不是一人所為」 — 因為受害者年齡、職業、教育、興趣差異太大，且性侵手法不同。但 Brussel 認為只有一人：

「他先攻擊象徵母親的長者；當他面對自己的伊底帕斯情結後，就能對年輕女性產生性反應 — 證據就是在現場留下精液。到最後一名受害者 Mary Sullivan，這個 strangler 已從『嬰兒期跳到青春期再到成年』，達到『瞬間成熟（instant maturity）』。」 — Brussel, 1968

Brussel 進一步描繪 strangler 為：偏執型思覺失調、肌肉結實、二十多到三十多歲、中等身高、五官無特殊、剃鬚乾淨、指甲乾淨、穿著整潔，並預測「未婚」。

6.3 Albert DeSalvo 與「綠衣人」

1964 年夏秋，波士頓地區發生多起女性遭性侵但未被殺害的案件。兇手因穿綠色工作褲被稱為 Green Man（綠衣人）。最後逮捕的是 Albert DeSalvo。他被送 Bridgewater 州立醫院後，向其他病患「炫耀」自己也是波士頓勒殺者。

DeSalvo 確實符合 Brussel 描述的人口統計大致樣貌 — 但他已婚。Brussel 後來說「未婚」是他「唯一的錯誤」 — 但這正是後見之明偏誤的痕跡。

🧬 DNA 顛覆：

2003 年的 DNA 與其他法醫證據 排除了 DeSalvo 涉入至少 Mary Sullivan 的命案（Sherman, 2003）。然而，Brussel 整個理論的關鍵正是「Sullivan 案後 strangler 達到性成熟，所以停手」。一旦 DeSalvo 被排除，Brussel 整套 Oedipal 詮釋的核心也隨之瓦解。

DeSalvo 從未因 Strangler 罪行受審。他僅因 Green Man 系列強姦案被判無期徒刑，1973 年在 Walpole 監獄被同囚刺殺身亡。

6 · The Boston Strangler (1962–1964)

6.1 Case Overview

  • From June 14, 1962 to January 4, 1964, 13 single women in the Boston area were murdered; at least 11 are attributed to the "Boston Strangler."
  • All were killed in their own apartments, sexually assaulted (sometimes with objects), bodies positioned in degrading poses.
  • All were strangled with their own clothing (stockings, belts).
  • No signs of forced entry — suggesting the victims may have known the killer.
  • The first 5 victims were 55–85; then a 21-year-old appeared, followed by women of varied ages.

6.2 Brussel's Freudian Reading

Most behavioral scientists at the time concluded "not one killer" — victims and methods varied too much. Brussel disagreed:

"He was first striking out at his mother, symbolized by the older women. Once he came to terms with his Oedipal complex, he could respond sexually to young women — evidenced by the semen left at the scene. By his last victim, 19-year-old Mary Sullivan, the strangler had 'suddenly grown, psychosexually, from infancy to puberty to manhood' — what I termed 'instant maturity'." — Brussel, 1968

Brussel further described him as a paranoid schizophrenic, muscular, late twenties to thirties, average height, clean-shaven, neatly dressed — and predicted he was unmarried.

6.3 Albert DeSalvo and the "Green Man"

In the summer-fall of 1964, a wave of rapes (without killings) hit the Boston area. The attacker — wearing green coveralls — was dubbed the Green Man. He turned out to be Albert DeSalvo, who, after being sent to Bridgewater State Hospital, boasted to fellow patients that he was also the Boston Strangler.

DeSalvo broadly matched Brussel's demographic description — but he was married. Brussel later called "unmarried" his "only mistake" — a textbook hindsight-bias move.

🧬 The DNA Twist:

In 2003, DNA and other forensic evidence cleared DeSalvo of at least Mary Sullivan's murder (Sherman, 2003). But Sullivan's death was the linchpin of Brussel's theory ("the strangler had achieved sexual maturity, so the killings would stop"). Clear DeSalvo of Sullivan, and Brussel's Oedipal framework collapses.

DeSalvo was never tried for the Strangler killings. He was convicted only of the Green Man rapes, given life imprisonment, and stabbed to death by another inmate at Walpole Prison in 1973.

本書把這個龐雜的領域整理成 5 個互有重疊但概念上可區分的範疇。請點下方任一卡片查看更多：

7 · The Five Areas of Behavioral Profiling

"Profiling" now overlaps with many labels — offender profiling, investigative analysis, behavioral analysis, criminal investigative analysis. The U.K. even uses behavioural investigative analyst (BIA) to escape the negative connotations of "profiling."

This book organizes the messy field into 5 overlapping but conceptually distinct categories. Click any card below for more:

1

犯罪現場側寫
Crime Scene Profiling

從現場證據推論未知嫌犯的認知傾向、行為模式、動機、情緒傾向與人口統計特徵。包含 linkage analysis（連結多起案件）。

核心限制：即使最精緻的版本，也鮮少能直接指認兇手 — 只能產生「合理的假設集」。

Crime Scene Profiling

Inferring an unknown offender's cognition, behavior patterns, motivation, emotional dispositions, and demographics from physical scene evidence. Includes linkage analysis (connecting cases).

Core limit: even sophisticated versions rarely identify the offender — they only generate a reasonable hypothesis set.

2

地理側寫
Geographic Profiling

從犯罪地點 & 彼此空間關係，估計嫌犯可能居住地或下次犯案地（Guerette, 2002）。聚焦兇手「熟悉、舒適、慣常出沒」的地理區域。

不僅用於連續暴力犯罪，也適用於連續竊盜等財產犯罪。

Geographic Profiling

Identifying an offender's likely residence or next-crime location from the spatial relationships among crime sites (Guerette, 2002). Focuses on territory the offender knows, feels comfortable in, and uses.

Useful not just for serial violent crime but also for serial burglary and other property offenses.

3

心理側寫
Psychological Profiling

多用於識別並預測危險個體（也可識別正面特質）。涵蓋兩個程序：

  • Threat assessment：評估特定威脅是否會落實的可信度／嚴重性（威脅來源可能未知）。
  • Risk assessment：評估某人對自己或他人造成傷害的機率。

本質上是「心理測驗與評估」的延伸（Canter & Alison, 2000）。

Psychological Profiling

Mainly used to identify and predict dangerous individuals (can also identify positive traits). Two procedures:

  • Threat assessment: judging whether a specific threat will be carried out (the source may be unknown).
  • Risk assessment: estimating the probability that a known person will harm self or others.

Essentially an extension of psychological testing and assessment (Canter & Alison, 2000).

4

嫌疑人式側寫
Suspect-Based Profiling

又稱 prospective profiling — 從歷史犯罪者資料中找出「人格／行為／認知／人口特徵」與某類犯罪的統計關聯，據以辨識可疑對象（如毒品走私者、校園槍手）。

⚠️ 爭議：當「群體特徵」採用種族／宗教／族裔時，就構成 racial profiling，可能違反憲法權利。2011 年 East Haven (CT) 警局即因此被 FBI 逮捕。

Suspect-Based Profiling

Also called prospective profiling — uses statistical links between offender characteristics (personality, behavior, cognition, demographics) and specific crime types (drug trafficking, school shootings, etc.) to flag suspects.

⚠️ Controversy: when the "group traits" include race, religion, or ethnicity, it becomes racial profiling and may violate constitutional rights. In 2011, the FBI arrested East Haven (CT) PD officers for exactly this.

5

可疑死亡分析
Equivocal Death Analysis

又稱 psychological autopsy（心理解剖）或 reconstructive psychological evaluation — 在死者過世後重建其情緒、行為、認知特徵。

常見場景：判定死亡為自殺、他殺或意外（保險理賠用、軍方自殺率調查 — 如 2011 年 7 月美陸軍創歷史新高 32 起自殺）。

Equivocal Death Analysis

Also called psychological autopsy or reconstructive psychological evaluation — reconstructing the emotional life, behavior patterns, and cognitive features of a deceased person.

Used to decide suicide vs. homicide vs. accident (insurance claims; military suicide investigations — e.g., U.S. Army's record 32 suicides in July 2011).

💡 觀念釐清：這五類雖然概念分明，但實際案件常會「跨界」 — 例如連續強姦案可能同時用到 crime scene profiling、geographic profiling 與 psychological profiling。後續章節會各自展開。
💡 Clarification: The five categories overlap in practice — a single serial-rape case might draw on crime scene, geographic, and psychological profiling at once. Each is unpacked in later chapters.

9 · 本章總結

  1. 「Profiling」是個古老但近年才被科學化的活動 — 從中世紀的女巫獵殺手冊一路到今天。
  2. 美國「現代側寫」始於 Mad Bomber 案 + Brussel 的公開介入；但仔細檢視，Brussel 的真正貢獻是把概念帶進大眾與 FBI 視野，而非具體破案技術。
  3. Barnum statements 與hindsight bias 是檢驗任何側寫聲稱時必須先檢查的兩個心理陷阱。
  4. 本書把 profiling 分為五大領域：crime scene、geographic、psychological（含 threat / risk）、suspect-based、equivocal death analysis。後面章節各自展開。
  5. 有效的側寫，必須整合經驗與判斷、理論、研究、專業共識（含倫理），並以良好設計的實證研究為核心。
🎯 一句話帶走：「真實世界沒有 Cracker」 — 側寫不是神蹟，它是一門需要持續實證檢驗的工具。

9 · Chapter Summary

  1. "Profiling" is an old activity but only recently scientific — from medieval witch-hunt manuals to today.
  2. Modern U.S. profiling began with the Mad Bomber case and Brussel's public involvement; but Brussel's real contribution was bringing the concept to the public and the FBI — not specific case-cracking techniques.
  3. Barnum statements and hindsight bias are the two cognitive pitfalls to check before believing any profiling claim.
  4. The book frames profiling as five overlapping areas: crime scene, geographic, psychological (threat/risk), suspect-based, and equivocal death analysis — each gets its own chapter.
  5. Effective profiling requires integrating experience and judgment, theory, research, and professional consensus (including ethics) — anchored in well-designed empirical research.
🎯 One-liner takeaway: "There is no Cracker in real life" — profiling isn't magic; it's a tool that demands ongoing empirical scrutiny.
