誤解真相
Profiler 會預測兇手「人格」人格預測的可信度低，且對警方而言常無用；應聚焦「行為指標」（如是否從事戶外活動）。

1 · Three Common Misconceptions (Rainbow & Gregory, 2011)

MisconceptionReality
Profilers predict offender personalityPersonality prediction has weak validity and is rarely helpful — focus on behavioral indicators.
Profilers solve crimesProfilers support investigation via behavioral science; self-promoting memoirs distort this.

美國哲學家 Charles Peirce 把人類獲得知識的方式分為四種。理解這四種，才能診斷自己的 profile 報告是「哪一種知識」：

2 · Peirce's Four Ways of Acquiring Knowledge

The American philosopher Peirce identified four ways. Understanding them helps diagnose what kind of knowledge a profile report contains:

①

Tenacity 固執法

「我一直都這樣相信」 — 不管證據怎麼說。這正是 belief persistence 的根，是四種裡最不可靠的。

Tenacity

"I've always believed this" — regardless of evidence. The root of belief persistence; the least reliable of the four.

②

Authority 權威法

「老師說」「FBI 說」「書上說」 — 高效但需被檢驗。法庭上的 expert testimony 也是這一類。

Authority

"My teacher said," "FBI says," "the book says" — efficient but needs scrutiny. Courtroom expert testimony falls here.

③

A priori 先驗法

「理性與邏輯演繹推出」 — 哲學與法律的基底。「常識（common sense）」也屬此 — 仰賴日常經驗。

A priori

"It stands to reason and logical deduction" — the basis of philosophy and law. "Common sense" lives here.

④

Science 科學法

系統性檢驗、實證、可修正、可推翻 — 「沒有絕對，只有機率」。Profiling 必須朝向這一種。

Science

Systematic testing, empirical, revisable, refutable — "no certainties, only probabilities." Profiling must move this way.

3 · Popper 的「可否證性（Falsifiability）」

Falsifiability — 可否證性

Popper：「一個陳述是科學的，前提是 — 它有可能被經驗推翻。」
「所有天鵝都是白的」無論你看過幾百萬隻白天鵝都不能證明它正確；但看到一隻黑天鵝就能否證它。

Profile 報告中的常見「不可否證」陳述：

「縱火是性興奮的替代，火焰的毀滅力反映了 pyromaniac 的性慾強度與虐待傾向。」
— Abrahamsen (1960)

這句話既不能被驗證也不能被否證，更無助於偵查 — 它違反 Popper 的科學標準。然而類似的精神分析式陳述至今仍出現於某些 profile 報告中。

📝 過去被 profiler 提出且後來被科學否證的「事實聲明」：
  • 「所有連續殺人犯都是白人」 — 假
  • 「美國以外幾乎沒有連續殺人犯」 — 假，全球證據累積中
  • 「大規模殺人犯幾乎都是精神病患」 — 假，多數臨床上並未達到診斷標準

3 · Popper's Falsifiability

Falsifiability

Popper: "A statement is scientific only if it could in principle be refuted by experience."
"All swans are white" can't be confirmed by any number of white swans — but one black swan refutes it.

Common "unfalsifiable" profile claims:

"Firesetting is a substitute for a sexual thrill, and the devastating and destructive powers of fire reflect the intensity of the pyromaniac's sexual desires, as well as his sadism."
— Abrahamsen (1960)

This sentence is neither verifiable nor falsifiable, and is useless to investigators — violating Popper's standard. Yet psychoanalytic claims like this still appear in some profile reports today.

📝 Past profiler claims since falsified by evidence:
  • "All serial killers are white" — false
  • "Serial killing barely happens outside the U.S." — false, global evidence accumulating
  • "Mass murderers are almost always insane" — false, most don't meet clinical criteria

4 · Canter 與 The Railway Rapist

1980 年代中期，倫敦及周邊鐵路站發生連續強姦案。1986 年 12 月首起命案（19 歲女性被綁離 Hackney Wick 站、強姦後勒斃），警方啟動英國自 Yorkshire Ripper 以來最大規模調查。Scotland Yard 邀請社會 / 環境心理學家 David Canter 介入。

FOCUS 3.1

John Duffy 與 David Mulcahy 雙人組

  • Duffy 自始即列嫌但被淹沒於 2,000+ 嫌犯中。
  • 關鍵線索：小腳印（只占英國成人男性 1%）、體型相符、髮色、痘疤、連帽衫與 balaclava 頭套。
  • Duffy 持刀強暴前妻是「把他從嫌犯名單中分離出來」的關鍵 — 但當時警員「假設家暴者不會去殺陌生人」。
  • 1987 年逮捕，1988 年判 6 個無期徒刑；1997 年才在獄中向 Cutler 醫師承認還犯下 20+ 起未被起訴的性侵。
  • 2000 年同夥 David Mulcahy 被判 15 罪 — 兩人少年時即一起虐待動物、玩氣槍打傷 4 人。

Canter 寫了 17 項人格與行為特徵 + 環境線索。該 profile 沒有直接破案，但成功指出 Duffy 居住的 Kilburn 區。更重要的：這個經驗讓 Canter 創立 investigative psychology 學科。

4 · Canter and the Railway Rapist

From the mid-1980s, women were attacked at railway stations in/around London. In December 1986, the first killing — a 19-year-old dragged from Hackney Wick Station, raped, strangled — triggered the largest U.K. investigation since the Yorkshire Ripper. Scotland Yard called in social/environmental psychologist David Canter.

FOCUS 3.1

John Duffy & David Mulcahy

  • Duffy was a suspect from the start but lost among 2,000+.
  • Key clues: a small footprint (1% of UK adult males), build, hair color, acne, hooded sweatshirt, balaclava.
  • Knife-raping his ex-wife separated him from the pack — but officers had assumed a "domestic abuser" wouldn't kill a stranger.
  • Arrested 1987, convicted 1988 (6 life sentences); confessed to 20+ additional rapes to Dr. Cutler in prison in 1997.
  • Accomplice David Mulcahy convicted on 15 counts in 2000 — both had tortured animals and shot 4 people with air rifles as teens.

Canter wrote a 17-trait profile + environmental cues. It didn't directly solve the case but correctly pinpointed Duffy's Kilburn neighborhood. Crucially: the experience led Canter to found investigative psychology.

5 · Paul Britton 與 Stagg 冤案的教訓

1992 年 Rachel Nickell 在 Wimbledon Common 推著 2 歲兒子散步時被刺死。心理學家 Paul Britton 採取「臨床／直覺」路線，告訴警方兇手「住附近、單身、對色情有興趣、有輕罪前科」 — 警方鎖定 Colin Stagg。

Britton 主動提議「honeytrap」：女警偽裝感興趣，逐步引誘 Stagg 自首。歷時數週，Stagg 從未自首。卻仍被羈押超過 1 年；最終獲釋並被警方道歉，真兇 Robert Napper 多年後才被起訴。

⚖️ 法律連鎖反應：
  • 2001 年英國 ACPO 把「offender profiler」改名為 Behavioural Investigative Analyst（BIA），並要求 ACPO 認證。
  • 第 9 章將回到本案討論可採性問題（Regina v. Stagg, 1994）。
  • Britton 一案後聲望大跌；profession 的科學化呼聲加大。

5 · The Stagg Cautionary Tale (Paul Britton)

In 1992, Rachel Nickell was stabbed dead on Wimbledon Common while walking with her 2-year-old. Psychologist Paul Britton's clinical/intuitive profile pointed to "single male, nearby, into pornography, prior minor offense" — police zeroed in on Colin Stagg.

Britton orchestrated a "honeytrap" — a female officer feigning interest to coax confession. Stagg never confessed. He was held over a year, then released; police later apologized. The true killer, Robert Napper, was prosecuted years afterward.

⚖️ Legal aftermath:
  • In 2001 the U.K. ACPO renamed "offender profiler" to Behavioural Investigative Analyst (BIA), requiring ACPO approval.
  • Chapter 9 returns to the admissibility issues (Regina v. Stagg, 1994).
  • Britton's standing fell sharply; calls for scientific profiling intensified.

6 · Canter 的四個研究問題

Canter (2000b) 主張：要把 profiling 變成扎實科學，必須有系統地回答下列四問。

  1. 現場有哪些行為特徵能幫助辨識兇手？（需 base rate + co-occurrence 資料）
  2. 如何辨識犯罪間／兇手間的差異？（unique themes、稀有行為）
  3. 能對兇手做出什麼推論？（從可區分的特徵建立人物推估）
  4. 有沒有其他案件也是同一人所為？（linkage analysis）

四個問題各自對應到「個別差異心理學」「環境心理學」「認知心理學」「社會心理學」既有的研究典範。

6 · Canter's Four Research Questions

Canter (2000b) argues that to make profiling solid science, four questions must be answered systematically:

  1. What scene behavioral features help identify the offender? (Needs base rates + co-occurrence data.)
  2. How to indicate differences between crimes/offenders? (Unique themes, rare behaviors.)
  3. What inferences identify the offender? (Build a person estimate from distinguishing traits.)
  4. Are there other crimes by the same person? (Linkage analysis.)

Each maps onto existing paradigms in individual differences, environmental, cognitive, and social psychology.

7 · A → C 方程式

🧠 Canter 的核心方程：
A — Actions
犯案前已知的所有現場行為
(victims, MO, signature, timing)
→
→ — Inference Process
科學與邏輯推論
(based on research, base rates)
→
C — Characteristics
兇手的可識別特徵
(demographics, psychology, geography)

關鍵在「→」 — 必須交代「為什麼能從 A 推到 C」。Alison 等 (2004) 統計大多數 profile 報告對此交代不清。報告應該說：

  • 這個推論的根據是什麼？（哪些研究？哪些 base rate？）
  • 推論的強度多強？（用百分比、機率）
  • 為什麼這個根據適用於本案？
FOCUS 3.3

BIA 模範報告大綱（Alison, West, & Goodwill, 2004）

  • Caveat / Disclaimer：強調是「偵查工具」、非用於指認無辜或有罪。
  • Competence and Qualifications：作者學經歷。
  • Peer Review：建議警方請其他法醫心理學家審查。
  • Case Summary and Evaluation：摘要、評估。
  • Evaluation of the Murder：用對照表比較嫌犯與相似類型兇手的特徵。
  • Evaluation of Suspect：基於文獻將嫌犯歸入某類強暴犯類型；用於 linkage。
  • Risk Assessment：以多項研究為基礎，謹慎地評估再犯。
  • Investigative Recommendations：聚焦特定地點、生活轉折期等。

7 · The A → C Equation

🧠 Canter's core equation:
A — Actions
All known crime-scene behaviors
(victims, MO, signature, timing)
→
→ — Inference
Scientific & logical processes
(grounded in research and base rates)
→
C — Characteristics
Identifying offender traits
(demographics, psychology, geography)

The crux is "→": the report must explain why A leads to C. Alison et al. (2004) found most profile reports do this poorly. Each inference should state:

  • What is the basis? (Which studies? Which base rates?)
  • How strong is the inference? (Use percentages.)
  • Why is this basis applicable here?
FOCUS 3.3

Model BIA Report Outline (Alison, West, & Goodwill, 2004)

  • Caveat / Disclaimer: "Investigative tool, not for innocence/guilt."
  • Competence and Qualifications: authors' biographies.
  • Peer Review: encourages review by other forensic psychologists.
  • Case Summary and Evaluation: details, evaluation.
  • Evaluation of the Murder: compares suspect traits to typology.
  • Evaluation of Suspect: classifies the suspect by literature on sex offending; supports linkage.
  • Risk Assessment: cautious, grounded in research.
  • Investigative Recommendations: focus areas (locations, life transitions).

8 · 從犯罪簽名到「心理簽名」

Ch 2 介紹的「signature」是兇手刻意留下的儀式性印記。Canter 學派提出更廣的概念：

類型定義例子
Crime scene signature有意識留下的儀式行為把屍體擺特定姿勢、留下符號
Psychological signature無意識的行為慣性 — 跨情境穩定講話節奏、用「她」而非「妳」、特定方言詞

📡 二戰經典案例（Gladwell 《Blink》）

英國情報員從不懂德文、卻能從 Morse code 的「敲擊節奏」聽出哪位德國電報員在發報，進而推估該部隊位置。德國電報員無意識地把人格特質寫進敲鍵節奏 — 這就是 psychological signature。

Bateman & Salfati (2007) 分析 90 名兇手的 450 起命案，發現「刻意」的 crime scene signature 並不一致 — 受情境影響太大；他們建議未來研究應聚焦在「內在心理流露」的 psychological signature。

8 · From Crime-Scene to Psychological Signature

Ch 2's "signature" was deliberate ritual behavior. The Canter school extends this:

TypeDefinitionExample
Crime scene signatureConscious ritual behaviorPosing the body; symbols
Psychological signatureUnconscious behavioral consistency across contextsSpeech cadence, "her" not "you," regional dialect

📡 WWII Case (Gladwell, Blink)

British interceptors couldn't read German Morse but could identify each German operator from keying cadence — locating units accordingly. The operators unconsciously encoded personality in their rhythm. That's psychological signature.

Bateman & Salfati (2007) examined 450 homicides by 90 offenders: deliberate scene signatures were inconsistent (too situation-driven). They urge future research on psychological signatures rooted in intrinsic psychology.

9 · 四個應該掌握的當代犯罪學理論

9 · Four Contemporary Theories You Must Know

A

Moffitt 發展路徑

LCP（life-course-persistent）：3 歲前出現問題、神經缺陷 + 家庭環境互動、終身犯罪、占人口 5–10%（女性 1–2%）但犯案率極高。
AL（adolescent-limited）：青少年期才出現，18–26 歲緩解。
對 profiling：連續犯多為 LCP，會在現有罪犯資料庫中 — 「不是要找新人，是要找對的舊人」。

Moffitt's Developmental Pathways

LCP: starts before age 3, neuropsych + family interaction, lifelong; 5–10% of population (females 1–2%), but accounts for the majority of crime.
AL: adolescence only, desists 18–26.
For profiling: serial offenders are usually LCP — already in databases.

B

Steinberg 青少年腦發展

前額葉（自我控制相關）約到 25 歲才完全成熟；衝動／賞酬尋求發展早，自我控制發展晚 — 解釋青少年高風險行為。
對 profiling：衝動、雜亂、高風險的犯罪 → 年輕犯（可能 24 歲以下）；如 "cowboy burglar"。

Steinberg's Adolescent Brain

Frontal cortex (self-control) matures ~age 25; reward-seeking peaks earlier than self-control — explaining teen risk-taking.
For profiling: impulsive, sloppy, high-risk scenes → young offenders (often under 24); the "cowboy burglar."

C

同儕拒絕與人際技能不足

5 歲時已具攻擊性的孩子常被同儕排擠；peer rejection 是日後嚴重暴力的強預測因子。
對 profiling：學業／少年紀錄雖難取得，但「青少年期是否被排擠」可能是有用的研判向度。

Peer Rejection & Deficient Interpersonal Skills

Aggressive 5-year-olds are excluded by peers; rejection strongly predicts later violence.
For profiling: juvenile records are hard to access, but peer-rejection patterns matter.

D

犯罪心理病態（Psychopathy）

占成人 < 5%，但其犯罪暴力比例極高。強姦犯中盛行率 40–50%；兒童性侵犯中 10–15%。Psychopath 的核心特質：四群組（人際印象管理／衝動不負責／情感缺損／反社會傾向）。
對 profiling：無動機、極端殘忍的暴力 → 從具廣泛前科的嫌犯名單中找具上述特徵者。

Psychopathy

< 5% of adults but a huge share of violent crime. 40–50% of rapists; 10–15% of child molesters. Four core trait clusters: impression mgmt, impulsive lifestyle, affective deficits, antisocial tendencies.
For profiling: motiveless, sadistic violence → look at extensive-record suspects with these traits.

10 · 即使「科學取徑」也躲不過的錯誤源

10.1 Heuristics（捷思）

Kahneman & Tversky 提醒：每個人都用「心智捷徑」 — 在時間壓力下尤其依賴。但同樣的捷徑可能釀成系統性錯誤。

捷思運作Profiling 風險
Representativeness把眼前案件套入「典型」類別「這像我幾年前處理的案」→ 忽略 base rate
Availability容易想起的越「重要」媒體大量報導的案件被當成「典型」

📰 Rainbow vs Britton

2010 年《Guardian》的訪談：Britton 對統計沒興趣；Rainbow 指出「84% 的強姦犯曾因其他罪被定罪、但很少是性犯罪」 — 這個事實「乏味卻真實」，正是 representative heuristic 容易忽略的 base rate。

10.2 Base Rate Fallacy

Rainbow 等 (2011) 90 歲老婦案經典：現場剝出的心臟、十字撥火棒、燭光、近德魯伊遺址 — 看起來像教派儀式。但 base rate 顯示「殺害長者多為熟識、住附近者」，加上一個足跡讓警方逮到 17 歲送報生。兇手刻意佈置（staging）誤導，但 base rate 救了偵查方向。

10.3 不確定性的表達

Heuer (1999)：「可能」一詞可指 1% 到 99%。Profile 應給具體百分比（如 "30% 機率"），否則警方會用 confirmation bias 填入自己的詮釋。

10 · Error Sources Even in the Scientific Approach

10.1 Heuristics

Kahneman & Tversky: everyone uses mental shortcuts — especially under pressure. Useful, but systematic errors follow.

HeuristicHow it worksProfiling risk
RepresentativenessFits the case into a "typical" category"Looks like my old case" → ignores base rates
AvailabilityEasily recalled = "important"Media-amplified cases treated as typical

📰 Rainbow vs Britton

In a 2010 Guardian piece: Britton dismissed statistics; Rainbow noted "84% of rapists have prior convictions, usually not for sexual offenses" — boring but true. The old-school profiler would ignore that base rate.

10.2 Base Rate Fallacy

Rainbow et al. (2011)'s 90-year-old woman case: removed heart on a silver tray, fireplace pokers as a crucifix, candles, near Druid ruins — looked occult. But base rates say elderly victims are killed by acquaintances nearby. A footprint plus the base rate led to the 17-year-old paperboy. The killer staged it to mislead — but base rates rescued the investigation.

10.3 Expressing Uncertainty

Heuer (1999): "could" can mean 1% or 99%. Profiles should give explicit percentages (e.g., "30%"); otherwise confirmation bias fills the gap.

12 · 本章總結

  1. Rainbow & Gregory 三大誤解（人格預測、破案、已是科學）告訴我們：profile 應該謙卑、行為導向、可被驗證。
  2. Peirce 四法 + Popper 可否證性，為 profiling 設立科學門檻。
  3. Canter 從 Railway Rapist 案開啟 investigative psychology；A→C 方程式要求每個推論交代「為什麼」。
  4. Britton-Stagg 冤案促成 ACPO BIA 制度與更嚴格的科學要求。
  5. Canter 四問 + 三類預測（anamnestic、actuarial、clinical）+ decision tree = 結構化專業判斷。
  6. Psychological signature 強調「無意識行為慣性」 — 跨情境穩定，可能比刻意 signature 更可靠。
  7. 四個當代犯罪學理論（Moffitt、Steinberg、Peer rejection、Psychopathy）應該整合進 profile 報告。
  8. Heuristics、base rate fallacy、模糊機率語言仍是科學取徑的潛在陷阱。
🎯 一句話帶走：「科學的 profile 不是『直覺套上術語』，而是『每個句子都能交代來源、強度、可否證性』。」

12 · Chapter Summary

  1. Rainbow & Gregory's three misconceptions — profiles should be humble, behavioral, testable.
  2. Peirce's four ways + Popper's falsifiability set the scientific bar.
  3. Canter's work on the Railway Rapist birthed investigative psychology; the A→C equation demands "why" for each inference.
  4. The Britton-Stagg miscarriage led to ACPO's BIA framework and tougher scientific standards.
  5. Canter's 4 questions + 3 prediction types (anamnestic, actuarial, clinical) + decision trees = structured professional judgment.
  6. Psychological signatures stress unconscious cross-situational consistency — potentially more reliable than deliberate signatures.
  7. Four contemporary theories (Moffitt, Steinberg, peer rejection, psychopathy) should be integrated into profile reports.
  8. Heuristics, base-rate fallacy, vague probability language remain traps even in scientific profiling.
🎯 One-liner takeaway: "A scientific profile isn't 'intuition with jargon' — every sentence cites a source, strength, and falsifiable claim."
