1 · FBI 側寫的起源 — Hoover 之後的解封

FBI 的前身於 1908 年成立；1924 年 J. Edgar Hoover 出任局長，1934 年正式更名為 FBI。Hoover 對心理學與社會科學公開「不感興趣」（Douglas & Olshaker, 1995）— 在他掌權近 50 年期間，心理學是「後勤」式的存在。他用心理戰術操控政壇，但拒絕設立公開的心理學單位。

1972 年 Hoover 過世是個分水嶺：限制鬆綁，同年 Quantico 新 FBI 學院落成，BSU 隨之誕生，將 criminology、psychology、sociology 整合為「行為科學」訓練核心。

1972

BSU 成立

FBI 學院 Quantico 校區開幕；BSU 整合行為科學進入聯邦執法訓練。

1972–1981

初期側寫案件

Pinizzotto (1984) 統計：BSU 在這 10 年間提供 192 件側寫協助。

1981

Douglas 創設專責側寫單位

當年接案 54 件，此後逐年攀升。

1984

NCAVC 成立

Reagan 總統下令設立國家暴力犯罪分析中心，由 FBI 督導；下含 BAU 與 ViCAP。

1995

Douglas 退休前

該機構每年分析超過 1,000 件案。

「我們不會在新鮮的犯罪現場走來走去就出現靈感或通靈閃光。這是個歸納與演繹推理、犯罪偵查經驗、犯罪行為知識、事實與統計機率交織的世界。」
— FBI 官網對 NCAVC 工作的官方描述

1 · FBI Origins — Unlocking After Hoover

The FBI's predecessor began in 1908; J. Edgar Hoover took charge in 1924, the agency was renamed in 1934. Hoover publicly disdained psychology and the social sciences (Douglas & Olshaker, 1995) — for nearly 50 years, psychology in the Bureau was a "back room" affair. He used psychological tactics on politicians but refused to create a public psychology unit.

Hoover's death in 1972 was the watershed. Restrictions eased, a new Academy opened at Quantico, and the BSU was born — integrating criminology, psychology, and sociology into "behavioral science" training for federal law enforcement.

1972

BSU established

The FBI Academy at Quantico opens; the BSU brings behavioral science into federal training.

1972–1981

Early caseload

Pinizzotto (1984): BSU provided profiling assistance on 192 cases.

1981

Douglas creates dedicated profiling unit

54 cases that year; caseload climbed every year after.

1984

NCAVC established

President Reagan creates the National Center for the Analysis of Violent Crime under the FBI, housing BAU and ViCAP.

1995

Douglas's retirement era

The agency was analyzing more than 1,000 cases per year.

"[Agents] do not get vibes or experience psychic flashes while walking around fresh crime scenes. Rather, it is an exciting world of investigation and research — a world of inductive and deductive reasoning; crime-solving experience; and knowledge of criminal behavior, facts, and statistical probabilities."
— FBI's official description of NCAVC work

3 · 1980 年兩篇開創論文

側寫真正進入美國執法界主流，靠的是 1980 年 FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin 中刊出的兩篇文章：

PAPER 1

Ault & Reese (1980) — "A Psychological Assessment of Crime Profiling"

用一個東海岸連續強姦案（2 年、7 名受害者）介紹側寫概念。BSU 提出嫌犯描述：白人男性、25–35 歲、離異或分居、勉強就業、高中學歷、自我形象差、住在案發附近、可能有偷窺癖。3 天後警方鎖定 40 名嫌犯，最終 1 週內逮捕真兇。

論文也提供「申請側寫須附上的資料清單」：完整現場照片、解剖報告、案件報告、證人訪談 — 至今仍是執法的標準操作。

關鍵金句：「Profiles are not the result of magical incantations and are not always accurate.」

PAPER 2

Hazelwood & Douglas (1980) — "The Lust Murderer"

引入兩個被全球廣為引用的概念：

  • Organized nonsocial offender：有條理、狡猾、可表現親和、犯案地點離家較遠。
  • Disorganized asocial offender：缺乏狡猾、反社會、關係困難、犯案地點靠近住處。

同時也指出「現場本身會反映兇手是哪一類」 — 衍生出「O/D crime scene」。但他們自己也承認此屬「藝術而非科學」(The process is an art and not a science)。

3 · The Two Seminal 1980 Papers

Profiling entered U.S. law enforcement's mainstream through two 1980 papers in the FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin:

PAPER 1

Ault & Reese (1980) — "A Psychological Assessment of Crime Profiling"

Used a 2-year, 7-victim serial rape case on the East Coast. BSU described the offender as: white male, 25–35, divorced/separated, marginally employed, high school education, poor self-image, living nearby, likely a peeping Tom. Within 3 days police narrowed to 40 suspects; arrested the true offender within a week.

The paper also laid out the checklist of materials a profiler needs — complete scene photos, autopsy, full report, witness interviews — still standard practice.

Key quote: "Profiles are not the result of magical incantations and are not always accurate."

PAPER 2

Hazelwood & Douglas (1980) — "The Lust Murderer"

Introduced two globally cited concepts:

  • Organized nonsocial offender: methodical, cunning, amiable, crimes at a distance from home.
  • Disorganized asocial offender: lacking cunning, aversion to society, relationship trouble, crimes close to home.

And crucially: the crime scene itself reflects the offender's category — giving us the "O/D crime scene." Even so, the authors admitted: "The process is an art and not a science."

4 · Organized vs Disorganized — 興起與被推翻

4.1 Douglas–Ressler 監獄訪談 (1979–1983)

Douglas 與 Ressler 在巡迴訓練各執法機構時，順道訪談被定罪的兇手。他們發現「訪到 10–12 個就看出端倪」，並與 Ann Burgess 教授合作申請國家司法研究院經費，由 Burgess 設計 57 頁問卷。專案名 Patterns of Homicide Crime Scene Project 最終訪談 36 位囚犯：

  • 24 名 organized（合計 97 名受害者）
  • 12 名 disorganized（合計 21 名受害者）
⚠️ 樣本限制 (Devery, 2010): 「即使是當時的連續殺人犯，這個樣本也可能不具代表性 — 它是便利樣本，只訪了願意說話的人。沒有對照組，無法說這些特徵在一般社區中有多常見。」此外，36 人中有些只殺過 1–2 人，更精確的稱呼是「性殺人犯」而非「連續殺人犯」。

4 · Organized vs Disorganized — Rise & Fall

4.1 The Douglas–Ressler Prison Interviews (1979–1983)

Touring training programs at law enforcement agencies, Douglas and Ressler interviewed convicted murderers in nearby prisons. After 10–12 they "could see something." Partnering with Prof. Ann Burgess (NIJ-funded, 57-page questionnaire), the Patterns of Homicide Crime Scene Project ultimately interviewed 36 inmates:

  • 24 organized (97 victims combined)
  • 12 disorganized (21 victims combined)
⚠️ Sample limits (Devery, 2010): "It is highly unlikely the sample was representative even of serial killers of their time — it was one of convenience. Without a control sample, characteristics cannot tell us how common they are in the general community." Some interviewees had killed only once or twice — better called "sexual murderers" than "serial killers."

4.3 Canter et al. (2004) 的關鍵反證

David Canter 等人從 Christopher Missen 連續殺人犯資料庫蒐集 100 名美國連續殺人犯的 39 個現場特徵，做 co-occurrence 分析：

  • 「organized」與「disorganized」的特徵很少同時出現於同一場景。
  • 除了極少數共現（如 70% organized 場景藏匿屍體+多現場），大多無法支持二分。
  • 結論：「being organized is typical of serial killers as a whole」 — 多數連續殺人犯都會展現「組織化」特徵，二分法本身缺乏實證基礎。

後續 Snook 等 (2008) 的回顧也未找到支持 O/D 的證據。CCM 1992 版本身已退讓為「continuum」描述，但 John Douglas 個人仍在其網站維持此分類。

💡 啟示：當一個工具在實務上「好用又直覺」，並不代表它在科學上有效。Mixed crime scene 的出現本身就洩漏二分法的問題。

4.3 Canter et al. (2004) — The Empirical Refutation

From the Christopher Missen archive, Canter and colleagues coded 39 scene aspects across 100 U.S. serial killers, testing co-occurrence:

  • "Organized" and "disorganized" features rarely co-occurred within the same scene.
  • Only two co-occurrence patterns reached respectable levels (e.g., body concealed + multiple scenes in 70% of organized cases).
  • Conclusion: "Being organized is typical of serial killers as a whole" — most serial killers display organized traits, so the dichotomy lacks empirical grounding.

Snook et al. (2008) found no supporting evidence either. The CCM (1992) itself retreated to a "continuum" description — yet John Douglas reportedly still endorses O/D on his personal site.

💡 Lesson: Practical and intuitive ≠ scientifically valid. The very existence of "mixed crime scenes" betrays the dichotomy's problem.

5 · Crime Classification Manual (CCM)

Douglas 等於 1992 年出版 Crime Classification Manual: A Standard System for Investigating and Classifying Violent Crimes（2006 再版）。這是 1980 年代以來 FBI 側寫研究的集大成，提供：

  • 各類罪行的定義特徵
  • 每類附案例（背景、受害者特徵、現場指標、法醫發現）
  • 2006 年新增：電腦犯罪、宗教極端謀殺、年長女性性殺害、跟蹤、兒童綁架
FOCUS 2.2

Cult Murder（教派謀殺）案例分析

CCM 定義 cult 為「成員對某些理念、物件或人物展現過度奉獻；其首要目標（如性、權力、金錢）並未為大多數成員所知」 — 即首腦清楚目的，但跟隨者不知情。當兩名以上成員犯下命案，即屬 cult murder。

  • 受害者：多為教派內部成員或邊緣成員。
  • 現場：可能有象徵性物件；屍體處置看目的 — 若要傳遞訊息則暴露，若要威懾內部則埋藏。
  • 案例：1990 年 Cleveland，Jeffrey Lundgren 領導的激進分裂教派殺害 Avery 一家五口，理由是「淨化獻祭」。Lundgren 於 2006 年被處死。

5 · Crime Classification Manual (CCM)

Douglas et al. published the CCM: A Standard System for Investigating and Classifying Violent Crimes in 1992 (re-issued 2006). It compiles 1980s-era FBI profiling research and provides:

  • Defining characteristics of each crime type
  • Case study per type (background, victimology, scene indicators, forensic findings)
  • 2006 additions: computer crimes, religious-extremist murder, elder female sexual homicide, stalking, child abductions
FOCUS 2.2

Cult Murder — Case Analysis

CCM defines a cult as "a body of adherents with excessive devotion… whose primary objectives of sex, power, and/or money are unknown to the general membership" — leadership knows, followers don't. When two+ members commit murder, it counts as cult murder.

  • Victims: usually cult members or peripheral members.
  • Scene: may contain symbolic items; bodies displayed (to send a message) or buried (to intimidate inner circle).
  • Case: Cleveland 1990 — Jeffrey Lundgren's radical splinter group killed the Avery family of five as "cleansing sacrifice." Lundgren was executed in 2006.

6 · 七個現場分析術語

讀任何側寫文獻或 CCM，你都會反覆看到下列術語。用「一句話 + 例子」記住它們：

術語白話定義 + 例子
Modus Operandi (MO) 「完成犯罪所需的手法」 — 可學習、可改變。連續竊賊找到新工具或破解新警報系統 → 改變 MO。
Personation 「超出完成犯罪所需的行為」 — 通常透露兇手心理需求。
Signature（簽名） 當 personation 出現於連續案件時即是 signature — 跨案重複、儀式性、近乎強迫的行為（如特定屍體擺位、特定符號）。比 MO 更穩定，更利於 linkage analysis。
Staging（佈置） 在警方到場前刻意改動現場。動機：(1) 引開偵查（如把家暴殺人偽裝成入侵竊盜）(2) 保護家屬尊嚴（把自慰窒息死亡擺成意外）。
Trophy（戰利品） 象徵兇手對受害者的勝利／征服 — 例：Jeffrey Dahmer 用福馬林保存身體部位。
Souvenir（紀念品） 讓兇手回憶犯罪愉悅的紀念物 — 例：拿走受害者珠寶。
Undoing（撫平） 兇手「心理上想撤回」犯行的行為 — 把屍體擺進床上、頭枕枕頭、蓋好棉被。通常與受害者有親近關係。
🔑 心智小結：MO 看「怎麼做」，Signature 看「為什麼這樣做」，Staging 看「想騙誰看」，Undoing 看「想騙誰心痛」。

6 · Seven Scene-Analysis Terms

Any profiling literature or the CCM will repeat these terms. Memorize with one-line + example:

TermPlain Definition + Example
Modus Operandi (MO) "The method needed to commit the crime" — learnable, changeable. Serial burglar finds a new tool → MO changes.
Personation "Behavior beyond what is needed to commit the crime" — usually reveals psychological needs.
Signature Personation that recurs across serial crimes — ritualistic, near-compulsive (specific body posing, symbols). More stable than MO; more useful for linkage analysis.
Staging Deliberate scene alteration before police arrive. Motives: (1) redirect investigation (e.g., disguising domestic murder as home invasion); (2) protect family dignity (autoerotic asphyxia death made to look accidental).
Trophy Symbolizes triumph over the victim — Jeffrey Dahmer preserved body parts in formaldehyde.
Souvenir A keepsake reminding the offender of the crime's pleasure — e.g., the victim's jewelry.
Undoing Behaviors trying to "undo" the crime psychologically — body placed in bed, head on pillow, covered. Usually a close relationship.
🔑 Mental Summary: MO = "how," Signature = "why this way," Staging = "to deceive whom about facts," Undoing = "to soothe whom emotionally."

7 · Case Linkage（案件連結分析）

用「跨案的行為相似性」推論「多起案件是同一人所為」。Fortin 案（本章開頭引言）即是 linkage analysis 引發法律爭議的代表。

有效連結的兩個必要條件

🧠 Linkage 需要同時成立：
跨案一致性
同一兇手在不同犯罪中行為穩定
(Behavioral Consistency)
+
跨人區別性
該行為要能區分這位兇手與其他兇手
(Distinctiveness)
→
有效連結
可以信任的同一兇手推論
(Valid Linkage)

Santtila 等 (2008) 分析義大利 23 名兇手的 116 起命案，發現「同一兇手行為一致、不同兇手間有差異」 — 支持 linkage 的核心假設。但 Bennell 等 (2012) 的綜述指出，「MO 的一致性僅中等」 — 因此 linkage 主要應靠 signature 而非 MO。

兩種應用方向

  1. 已知一案 → 找出資料庫中相似案件（探勘未知兇手）
  2. 已知嫌犯 + 一案 → 找出可能也是他做的其他案件（搜尋未破案件）

7 · Case Linkage

Uses behavioral similarity across crimes to infer "same offender." The Fortin case (chapter epigraph) is a classic legal battleground for linkage analysis.

Two Necessary Conditions

🧠 Linkage requires both:
Within-Offender Consistency
The same offender behaves similarly across crimes
+
Between-Offender Distinctiveness
That behavior distinguishes this offender from others
→
Valid Linkage
Trustworthy inference of common authorship

Santtila et al. (2008) examined 116 Italian homicides by 23 offenders, confirming within-consistency + between-distinctiveness. But Bennell et al. (2012)'s review shows MO consistency is only moderate — so linkage should lean on signatures, not MO.

Two Use Cases

  1. From a known case → search the database for similar ones (offender unknown).
  2. From a suspect + one case → search unsolved cases potentially his.

8 · Clinical vs Actuarial 兩種取徑

🩺 Clinical（臨床式）
  • ● 倚重經驗、訓練、直覺、主觀
  • ● 強調進入兇手心理
  • ● FBI 主流
  • × 易受 confirmation bias、自我服務偏誤等影響
  • × 方法不公開、難以驗證
📊 Actuarial（精算式）
  • ● 倚重統計 base rate、過去類似個案的群體機率
  • ● Canter 等英國學派
  • ● 可重現、可實證檢驗
  • × 較為「乾燥無趣」、缺乏戲劇感
  • × 仍需要好品質的歷史資料
Base Rate（基本率）

「某行為在特定群體中、在特定時期（通常一年）內的盛行率。」是 actuarial 的核心。例：保險公司知道「20 歲男大生、學業中等、新運動車」群體 1 年內肇事率 40% → 因此保費較高。

Harcourt (2007) 提醒我們：base rate 早已滲透刑事司法 — 從 IRS 稅務稽查、毒品走私側寫、機場與街頭攔查、保釋羈押決定、刑期長度、監獄分級、假釋資格等，「預測工具越來越多地決定個人的法律結果」。

8 · Clinical vs Actuarial

🩺 Clinical
  • ● Experience, training, intuition, subjectivity
  • ● Tries to enter the offender's mind
  • ● Dominant in the FBI
  • × Vulnerable to confirmation bias, self-serving bias
  • × Methods not disclosed, hard to validate
📊 Actuarial
  • ● Statistical base rates, group probabilities from prior cohorts
  • ● Canter and the U.K. school
  • ● Reproducible, empirically testable
  • × "Dry," lacks drama
  • × Still needs quality historical data
Base Rate

"Statistical prevalence of a behavior in a given group over a set period (usually one year)." Core to actuarial profiling. Insurance: 20-year-old male college student, mediocre grades, new sports car → ~40% accident rate/year → higher premium.

Harcourt (2007) warns: base rates already permeate criminal justice — IRS Discriminant Index, drug-courier and racial profiles, airport/highway/street stops, pretrial detention, sentence length, prison classification, parole eligibility. "Prediction instruments increasingly determine individual outcomes in our policing, law enforcement, and punishment practices."

9 · Profile 的準確度與實用性

Gekoski & Gray (2011) 強調：「準確 ≠ 有用」。一份「事後看是準的」profile 也可能毫無實用價值 — 因為警方早就靠例行偵查得出相同結論。

9.1 三個經典研究結論一覽

研究樣本主要結論
Pinizzotto & Finkel (1990) 28 人：4 FBI 專家、6 受訓偵探、6 未受訓偵探、6 臨床心理師、6 大學生 受訓 profilers 在性犯罪案略佔優勢，但命案無明顯優勢；專家處理資訊方式與非專家無顯著差異。建議：團隊比單人更有效。

🧬 DNA 會取代 profiling 嗎？(Focus 2.3)
隨著 CODIS、AFIS、NIBIN 等資料庫成熟，DNA 偵測量遠超實驗室處理能力。Gekoski & Gray (2011) 推測未來 DNA 可能讓 profiling「過時」。但心理學處理的是「機率」而非「確定性」 — 在無 DNA 的案件、或需要理解動機與下一步行為時，behavioral profiling 仍有價值。

9 · Accuracy vs Usefulness

Gekoski & Gray (2011) emphasize: accurate ≠ useful. A post-hoc "accurate" profile may add nothing — police might have reached the same conclusion via routine work.

9.1 Three Classic Studies

StudySampleMain Finding
Pinizzotto & Finkel (1990) 28 people: 4 FBI experts, 6 trained detectives, 6 untrained, 6 clinical psychologists, 6 students Trained profilers slightly better on sex offense; no advantage on homicide; experts did not process info differently. Recommends team-based profiling.
Copson (1995) 184 U.K. cases, 29 profilers Helped solve only 14.1%; identified the offender in only 2.7%; yet 61% helped "understand the offender" and 82.6% of detectives felt "somewhat useful." Profiler reputation drove perceived usefulness.
Snook et al. (2007) meta-analysis Pooled empirical studies Profilers do not decisively outperform other groups; call profiling a "pseudoscientific technique" until empirically validated on large samples of active profilers.
🧬 Will DNA Make Profiling Obsolete? (Focus 2.3)
With CODIS, AFIS, and NIBIN databases, DNA demand outstrips lab capacity. Gekoski & Gray (2011) speculate DNA may eventually displace profiling. But psychology trades in probabilities, not certainties — for cases without DNA, or when investigators need to understand motive and next moves, behavioral profiling retains value.

10 · 三大認知陷阱 — 為什麼 profiler 常常失準？

Herbert Simon (1957) 的 Bounded Rationality（有限理性）提醒我們：每個人都建構自己的「簡化心智模型」並在其中運作。Profiler 無法真正進入兇手的心 — 他只能用自己的模型去模擬。Heuer (1999) 將此推到情報分析領域，並列出三個關鍵陷阱：

10 · The Three Cognitive Pitfalls

Herbert Simon's (1957) Bounded Rationality: each of us constructs a simplified mental model and operates within it. A profiler can't really enter the offender's mind — they only simulate it via their own model. Heuer (1999) extended this to intelligence analysis and named three key pitfalls:

A

Confirmation Bias（確認偏誤）

「只看支持自己預設的證據，忽略反證。」Profiler 與偵查員都會在曖昧的 profile 中挑出符合自己假設的部分。Copson (1995) 發現 50% 以上 profile 被認為「有用」其實是因為「強化了警方原本的看法」。

加劇條件 (Ask & Granhag, 2005)：偵辦時間壓力 + 強調決斷力的警察文化 + 重大決策牽涉聲譽。

Confirmation Bias

"Cherry-picking evidence that confirms prior beliefs while ignoring contradictions." Both profilers and detectives selectively read ambiguous profiles to match their hunches. Copson (1995) found 50%+ of profiles deemed "useful" simply reinforced officers' beliefs.

Aggravating conditions (Ask & Granhag, 2005): time pressure + decisive police culture + decisions tied to reputation.

B

Self-Serving Bias（自利偏誤）

「成功歸己、失敗歸境」 — 以維持高自尊與專業形象。Brussel 在 Mad Bomber、Boston Strangler 案的回憶錄即為經典 — 他堅稱只「犯一個錯」（預測未婚實為已婚），卻略過大量未提的錯誤預測。

這也是為何 profiling 領域充滿勝利敘事的回憶錄 — 出版誘因+自利偏誤=只記得對的。

Self-Serving Bias

"Credit success to self, blame failure on circumstance" — to maintain self-esteem and professional standing. Brussel's memoirs on the Mad Bomber and Boston Strangler are the textbook case — claiming "only one mistake" (predicting unmarried when he was married) while ignoring many other misses.

It also explains why profiling literature is full of triumph memoirs — publishing incentives + self-serving bias = only the hits get remembered.

C

Fundamental Attribution Error（基本歸因謬誤）

「高估人格(內在)、低估情境(外在)。」FBI 傳統 profiling 假設「現場行為穩定反映人格」 — 完全是 FAE 的展現。

但事實上，受害者反應、武器、場地結構等外部變項都會劇烈影響兇手在那個當下的行為。Mischel & Peake (1982) 早已指出：人類行為跨情境的不一致性比想像中大。

反例：Douglas & Olshaker 引用的「Homicidal Triad（殺人三聯徵）」 — 尿床、虐待動物、縱火 — 並未被實證支持，僅「虐待動物」與後續暴力有部分相關。這是 FAE 把「相關」當成「因果」的代表。

Fundamental Attribution Error

"Overweight personality (internal); underweight situation (external)." Classic FBI profiling assumes "scene behavior stably reflects personality" — pure FAE.

Yet victim reaction, weapon availability, scene layout heavily shape what an offender does in the moment. Mischel & Peake (1982) showed long ago that cross-situational consistency is weaker than assumed.

Cautionary example: Douglas & Olshaker's "Homicidal Triad" (bedwetting, cruelty to animals, firesetting) is not empirically supported; only "cruelty to animals" weakly predicts later violence. A textbook case of FAE mistaking correlation for causation.

12 · 本章總結

  1. 美國 crime scene profiling 始於 Hoover 過世後 1972 年的 BSU；1984 年 NCAVC 成立後組織化為 BAU 三組 + ViCAP。
  2. 1980 年兩篇開創論文（Ault & Reese；Hazelwood & Douglas）把 profiling 帶進主流，並奠定了 O/D 二分法。
  3. Douglas–Ressler 36 人監獄訪談的「便利樣本」性質決定了其推論的脆弱性；Canter 等 (2004) 的 100 人研究即推翻了 O/D 的統計基礎。
  4. 七個關鍵術語（MO、Signature、Personation、Staging、Trophy、Souvenir、Undoing）是讀任何 profile 報告的入場券。
  5. Case linkage 必須同時滿足「跨案一致 + 跨人區別」 — signature 比 MO 更可靠。
  6. Clinical 與 actuarial 兩種取徑各有優缺；FBI 主導 clinical，Canter 主導 actuarial。
  7. 三大實證研究（Pinizzotto & Finkel 1990、Copson 1995、Snook 2007 meta-analysis）告訴我們：profilers 的直接破案率極低、實用性主要來自「強化偵查員直覺」、整體仍接近 pseudoscience。
  8. 三大認知陷阱（confirmation bias、self-serving bias、fundamental attribution error）是 profiler 失準的根源。
🎯 一句話帶走：「Profiling 比警察工作多了一層心理視角，但也多了一層認知陷阱。」

12 · Chapter Summary

  1. U.S. crime scene profiling started with the BSU in 1972 post-Hoover; the 1984 NCAVC organized it into BAU's three units + ViCAP.
  2. The 1980 papers (Ault & Reese; Hazelwood & Douglas) mainstreamed profiling and seeded the O/D dichotomy.
  3. The Douglas–Ressler 36-inmate "convenience sample" set the stage; Canter et al. (2004) on 100 killers refuted O/D's statistical basis.
  4. Seven scene-analysis terms (MO, Signature, Personation, Staging, Trophy, Souvenir, Undoing) are your admission ticket to any profile report.
  5. Case linkage requires both within-offender consistency AND between-offender distinctiveness — signature beats MO.
  6. Clinical (FBI) vs actuarial (Canter) each have trade-offs.
  7. Three empirical landmarks (Pinizzotto & Finkel 1990; Copson 1995; Snook 2007 meta-analysis): profilers' direct solve rate is low, "usefulness" mostly reinforces detective hunches, the field remains near-pseudoscience.
  8. Three cognitive pitfalls (confirmation, self-serving, FAE) underlie why profilers miss the mark.
🎯 One-liner takeaway: "Profiling adds a psychological lens to police work — and one more layer of cognitive traps."
