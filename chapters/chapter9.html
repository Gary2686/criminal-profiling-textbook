1 · 兩條進法庭的路徑（Canter et al. 2004）

👤 P-DC（Profile-Defendant Correspondence）

把被告與 profile 對照 — 「他符合或不符合 X 類兇手的特徵」。爭議性最高，因直接針對特定被告，prejudicial 風險大。

🔗 P-CC（Profile-Crime Correspondence）

把多起案件對照 — 「兩件犯罪手法相似，疑為同一人所為」。即 linkage analysis。對被告偏見較低，但科學基礎仍受質疑。

還有第三種：profile-suspect correspondence

警察申請搜索令或逮捕令時，使用 profile 描述「嫌犯可能特徵」。如 U.S. v. Sokolow (1989) 中 drug courier profile 用於攔停。最高法院允許其用於偵查階段，但禁止用於審判階段作為有罪證據。

💡 共識：「Profiler 不應斷言被告有罪」是國際近乎共識。爭議在「能否描述類型特徵」與「動機分析」的界線。

1 · Two Courtroom Paths (Canter et al., 2004)

👤 P-DC (Profile-Defendant Correspondence)

Compares the defendant against a profile — "He does/doesn't fit type-X offender." Most controversial — directly aimed at a specific defendant; highest prejudicial risk.

🔗 P-CC (Profile-Crime Correspondence)

Compares multiple cases — "Two crimes look similar; possibly the same offender." This is linkage analysis. Less bias against the defendant, but its science is still questioned.

Plus a third: profile-suspect correspondence

Police request search/arrest warrants using profiles. E.g., U.S. v. Sokolow (1989) used the drug courier profile for stops. The Supreme Court allowed it at the investigative stage but barred trial use as proof of guilt.

💡 Consensus: "Profilers should not assert defendant guilt" — near-universal. The disputes are about describing type characteristics and motivational analysis.

2 · 標準演化：Frye → Daubert 三部曲

1923

Frye v. United States

原告涉早期 polygraph 測試。法院首創「general acceptance（通用接受）」標準：科學技術必須在相關領域中已普遍接受。一直主導到 20 世紀末，現仍在許多州法院、UK、加拿大、澳洲使用。

1993

Daubert v. Merrell Dow Pharmaceuticals

民事案件（Bendectin 抗孕吐藥致畸胎指控）。聯邦最高法院把法官設為 gatekeeper（守門人）。四大判準：

  1. Falsifiability（可否證性）
  2. Known or potential error rate（已知或潛在錯誤率）
  3. Peer review and publication（同儕審查與發表）
  4. General acceptance（保留 Frye 但不再是唯一標準）
1997

General Electric Co. v. Joiner

確認 「上訴法院不應隨意推翻已仔細審查證據的初審法官」。確立法官守門人地位。

1999

Kumho Tire Co. v. Carmichael

把 Daubert 標準擴展到「科學的、技術的、與其他專業知識」 — 心理學受同等審查。

但法官真的懂嗎？

Gatowski et al. (2001) 訪 400 名州法官：

  • 只有 ~5% 理解 error rate 與 falsifiability 概念。
  • 最常考慮的因素仍是：(1) 專家資格 (2) 相關性 (3) 通用接受度 (4) 通用可靠性。
  • error rate / falsifiability 鮮少被提及。

Probative vs Prejudicial — 即使通過科學門檻仍須權衡

所有證據規則都要求probative（證明力）> prejudicial（偏見力）。即使證據通過 Daubert，仍可能因偏見過高而被排除。

2 · Standards Evolution: Frye → Daubert Trilogy

1923

Frye v. United States

Involved an early polygraph. Established the "general acceptance" standard: scientific techniques must have gained general acceptance in the field. Dominated until late 20th century; still used in many U.S. states, UK, Canada, Australia.

1993

Daubert v. Merrell Dow Pharmaceuticals

A civil case (Bendectin antinausea drug birth-defect allegations). The Supreme Court made the judge a gatekeeper. Four criteria:

  1. Falsifiability
  2. Known or potential error rate
  3. Peer review and publication
  4. General acceptance (kept but no longer sole)
1997

General Electric Co. v. Joiner

Confirmed appellate courts should not lightly second-guess trial judges who carefully reviewed evidence. Locked in gatekeeper status.

1999

Kumho Tire Co. v. Carmichael

Extended Daubert to "scientific, technical, and other specialized knowledge" — psychology gets the same scrutiny.

But Do Judges Really Get It?

Gatowski et al. (2001), 400 state judges:

  • Only ~5% understood error rate and falsifiability.
  • Most-relied factors: (1) expert qualifications (2) relevance (3) general acceptance (4) general reliability.
  • Error rate / falsifiability rarely cited.

Probative vs Prejudicial — Still Weighed Even After Daubert

All evidence rules require probative > prejudicial. Even Daubert-admissible evidence can be excluded if too prejudicial.

3 · Rachel Nickell 案（R. v. Stagg, 1994）— 英國的轉折點

FOCUS 9.1

「The Bane of Our Lives」（我們專業的禍根）— Canter 語

1992 年 7 月，23 歲的 Rachel Nickell 在 Wimbledon Common 推著 2 歲兒子散步時，被刺 49 刀致死。心理學家 Paul Britton 採取 Brussel 式「視覺化犯罪」取徑：

  • 「單身男性，與父母同住，距 Wimbledon Common 走路可達」
  • 「擁有色情品收藏」
  • 「過去有輕微性犯罪，其偏差會逐漸升級」

Colin Stagg 住附近、曾被警告（在 Common 上裸體日光浴、寫過猥褻信給陌生女子）、案發當日帶狗在 Common 散步、承認擁有色情雜誌。

Honeytrap 操作

警方依 Britton 建議派女警喬裝接近 Stagg，「鼓勵」他寫越來越露骨的性幻想信。Britton 認為 Stagg 信中有「sadistic 元素」 — 警方逮捕。

法官的話

「將心理側寫於任何情況下作為身分證明，皆充滿可觀的危險。」
「我不希望鼓勵偵查或起訴單位以此種基礎來建構或補強案件。」

法官駁回 profiler 上證人台。Stagg 被羈押 14 個月後在審判第一天獲釋。真兇 Robert Napper 在案件被誤指期間又殺害一對母女。2008 年倫敦市警局正式向 Stagg 道歉。

Canter 把此案稱為「我們專業的禍根」 — 它讓英國法院對 profiling 法庭採信永久懷疑。

3 · The Rachel Nickell Case (R. v. Stagg, 1994) — UK's Turning Point

FOCUS 9.1

"The Bane of Our Lives" — Canter

July 1992: 23-year-old Rachel Nickell was stabbed 49 times on Wimbledon Common while walking with her 2-year-old son. Psychologist Paul Britton took a Brussel-style "visualization" approach:

  • "Single man, lives with parents, walking distance to Wimbledon Common"
  • "Owns pornography collection"
  • "Has a minor prior sexual offense; deviancy will escalate"

Colin Stagg lived nearby, had been cautioned (nude sunbathing on the Common, obscene letter to a stranger), was walking his dog on the Common that day, owned porn magazines.

The Honeytrap

On Britton's advice, an undercover female officer was sent to encourage Stagg to write increasingly explicit fantasies. Britton said Stagg's letters showed "sadistic elements" — police arrested.

The Judge's Words

"The notion of psychological profiling in any circumstances as proof of identity is redolent of considerable danger."
"I don't wish to give encouragement to investigating or prosecuting authorities to seek to construct or supplement a case on this kind of basis."

The judge barred profiler testimony. Stagg, held 14 months, was freed on the first day of trial. The real killer, Robert Napper, killed another mother and her 4-year-old daughter during Stagg's wrongful focus. In 2008 the Met Police formally apologized.

Canter called this case "the bane of our lives" — it cemented British court skepticism of profiling.

4 · 現場分析證言：法院容忍度較高

對「現場可觀察事實」（MO、signature、staging、O/D 分類）的證言，法院相對寬容 — 因為這在偵查員專業範圍內。

判例結論
U.S. v. Meeks (1992)軍事法庭認可「crime scene analysis 是超出一般人知識的專業領域」；允許 FBI Judson Ray 證稱「兇手帶著性與殺害的心思到場」。
State v. Wallace (2000) NC被告 9 件命案；Ressler 證稱為「mixed」型；辯方強調無組織面、檢方強調有組織面（暗示無精神疾患）。死刑判決維持。
State v. Lowe (1991) Ohio初審法院排除 John Douglas 證言；上訴法院維持，認為其分析「基於現場經驗的直覺，但不足以證明可靠性」。Daubert 後法官守門人立場明顯。
R. v. Ranger (2003) Canada允許 Detective Lines 證稱「現場為偽裝闖空門」但禁止其推論兇手動機與「最可能嫌疑人特徵」 — 「criminal profiling 不過是受過教育的猜測」。

4 · Crime Scene Testimony: Higher Court Tolerance

Courts are more permissive of testimony about observable scene facts (MO, signature, staging, O/D) — this falls within investigators' domain.

CaseHolding
U.S. v. Meeks (1992)Military court: 'crime scene analysis is specialized knowledge beyond the ken of the average person'; allowed FBI's Judson Ray to testify 'the offender came with sex and killing on his mind.'
State v. Wallace (2000) NC9 murders; Ressler called the scenes 'mixed'; defense stressed disorganized features, prosecution emphasized organized (implying no mental illness). Death sentence stood.
State v. Lowe (1991) OhioTrial court excluded John Douglas; appellate court upheld — his analysis was 'intuitiveness honed by experience but not sufficient evidence of reliability.' Post-Daubert gatekeeper at work.
R. v. Ranger (2003) CanadaAllowed Detective Lines's staging testimony; barred her motive and 'likely suspect' inferences — 'criminal profiling amounts to no more than educated guesses.'

5 · Linkage Analysis 在法庭

5.1 State v. Pennell (Delaware 1989)

三名女性被酷刑與謀殺；其中兩件由物證直接連結到 Pennell，第三件可能是「copycat」。FBI 的 John Douglas 在法庭證稱「現場有共同 signature」連結三案。Pennell 被定罪。

📌 重點：Delaware 最高法院基於 Douglas 多年 FBI 經驗而非科學標準允許其證言。同時強烈反對「profile」式證據作為偏見過高。
George (2008)：「FBI 探員為了能上法庭，刻意把 profiling 改稱為 linkage analysis」 — 術語包裝的力量。

5.2 State v. Fortin (New Jersey 2000, 2007)

本書第 2 章已提及。Steven Fortin 因兩件相似案件被連結：Melissa Padilla 命案（NJ 1994） + Maine 州警遭性侵案（1995）。前 FBI Roy Hazelwood 在法庭證稱「兩案如此獨特，只可能是同一人」。

📉 NJ 最高法院的判決：
  • Linkage analysis「過於玄奧、未經同儕檢驗」 — 不符合 general acceptance。
  • Hazelwood 可基於 FBI 經驗證稱「兩案相似性」，但不可證稱「必為同一人所為」。
  • Risinger & Loop (2002)：法院呈現「split personality」 — 拒絕 linkage 之名、允許 linkage 之實。

Fortin 再次被定罪、仍在 NJ 死刑犯名單。Risinger & Loop 銳利批評：「Linkage analysis 並非為偵查所開發，而是為了把『其他犯罪』證據納入法庭，加強對已被認定有罪之被告的不利印象。」

5 · Linkage Analysis in Court

5.1 State v. Pennell (Delaware 1989)

Three women tortured and killed; physical evidence linked Pennell to two, the third could have been a copycat. FBI's John Douglas testified to a 'common signature' linking all three. Pennell was convicted.

📌 Key: The Delaware Supreme Court allowed Douglas's testimony based on FBI experience rather than scientific standards — but strongly opposed 'profile' evidence as prejudicial.
George (2008): "FBI agents reframed profiling as 'linkage analysis' to gain courtroom admission" — terminology framing matters.

5.2 State v. Fortin (New Jersey 2000, 2007)

Ch 2 introduced this case. Fortin was linked via two similar attacks: the Melissa Padilla murder (NJ 1994) + the Maine state trooper sexual assault (1995). Roy Hazelwood testified: 'the two crimes are so unique only one person could have done both.'

📉 NJ Supreme Court ruling:
  • Linkage analysis is 'too esoteric and untested by peers' — fails general acceptance.
  • Hazelwood may testify on FBI experience-based 'similarity' but NOT 'same offender.'
  • Risinger & Loop (2002): the court shows a 'split personality' — rejected linkage by name, allowed it in substance.

Fortin was reconvicted and remains on NJ death row. Risinger & Loop's cutting critique: "Linkage analysis was developed not as an investigative aid, but to admit other-crime evidence and reinforce the jury's case against already-believed-guilty defendants."

6 · 動機與人格分析：最危險的領域

當 profiler 從現場可觀察事實「跨越」到推論動機、人格特質、心理特徵時，法院最為警惕 — 這已超出執法經驗範圍，且偏見值高。

正反案例比較

案例處理
Simmons v. State (2000)BAU 探員 Thomas Neer 證稱「兇手動機為性快感」 — 因該動機是死刑資格要件而被允許。但法院承認「需限制陪審團對證言的考慮」。
State v. Haynes (1988)「Anger-retaliatory」murder 證言被排除：「不科學、不可靠、prejudicial > probative，等同人格證據」。
R. v. Mohan (Canada 1994)排除「psychosexual profile」 — 「科學社群尚未為該類犯罪建立標準 profile」。
R. v. Klymchuk (Canada 2005)推翻定罪：FBI Brantley「動機」證言「無法經受嚴格科學審查」；流行文化讓 profiler 形象「幾乎強迫陪審團接受」。
State v. Sorabella (Connecticut 2006)Ken Lanning 可描述 preferential vs situational 性犯罪者；但不可說 Sorabella 符合任一類。
FOCUS 9.4

Drake v. Portuondo (2003) — 偽造資歷的 profiler

Richard Walter 在 Drake 雙重命案中：

  • 聲稱「做過數千件 profile」 — 誇大
  • 聲稱是大學「兼任教授」 — 其實僅一次客座演講
  • 診斷「picquerism」（持刀切割帶來性快感）— 此概念在精神醫學界備受爭議
  • 從未訪談 Drake 即下診斷

Drake 被定罪。獄中上訴 20 年。2003 上訴法院認為 Walter 證詞「misleading」但非「perjurious」。2009 再次因檢方不當再審 — 仍被定罪。Walter 被 American Academy for Forensic Sciences 調查但獲清白。

本案凸顯：法院對自我宣稱的 profiler 資格驗證機制嚴重不足。

CASE

People v. Masters (2001) — 最沉重的教訓

1987 年女子被姦殺；案發時 15 歲的鄰居 Tim Masters 路過未報警。10 年後（1998）警方提供心理學家 J. Reid Meloy 該少年的短篇故事與塗鴉。Meloy 從未訪談 Masters，但下結論：「沸騰中的潛在暴力鍋」、「補償性自戀幻想世界」。

Masters 被定罪入獄。2008 年新 DNA 證據出現，定罪被推翻；2011 大陪審團駁回所有指控。Meloy 後表示：警方曾隱瞞關鍵證據，他不會以那樣的方式作證。

無辜者坐了將近 10 年牢。

6 · Motivation & Personality: The Most Dangerous Territory

When profilers cross from observable scene facts into motive, personality, or psychological characteristics, courts grow most wary — this exceeds law-enforcement expertise and is high in prejudice.

For and Against

CaseHandling
Simmons v. State (2000)BAU's Thomas Neer testified 'motive = sexual gratification' — admitted because it bore on death-penalty eligibility; court limited jury consideration.
State v. Haynes (1988)'Anger-retaliatory' testimony excluded: 'not scientific, not reliable, more prejudicial than probative, akin to character evidence.'
R. v. Mohan (Canada 1994)Excluded a psychosexual profile — 'scientific community has not established a standard profile for that type of offender.'
R. v. Klymchuk (Canada 2005)Conviction overturned: Brantley's motive testimony 'cannot withstand rigorous scientific scrutiny'; pop culture gives profilers a status 'all but demanding acceptance.'
State v. Sorabella (Connecticut 2006)Ken Lanning could describe preferential vs situational sex offenders; could NOT say Sorabella fit either.
FOCUS 9.4

Drake v. Portuondo (2003) — The Credentials Inflater

Richard Walter on Drake's double-homicide:

  • Claimed 'thousands of profiles' — inflated
  • Claimed an 'adjunct professorship' — actually a single guest lecture
  • Diagnosed 'picquerism' (sexual pleasure from stabbing) — a contested concept
  • Never interviewed Drake before diagnosing

Drake convicted. 20 years of appeals. 2003 appellate court called Walter's testimony 'misleading,' not 'perjurious.' 2009 new trial for prosecutorial misconduct — convicted again. Walter was investigated by AAFS but cleared.

The case highlights: courts severely lack mechanisms to verify self-claimed profiler credentials.

CASE

People v. Masters (2001) — The Heaviest Lesson

A woman was raped and murdered in 1987; 15-year-old neighbor Tim Masters walked past the body without reporting. 10 years later (1998) police provided psychologist J. Reid Meloy with the teen's short stories and doodles. Meloy never interviewed Masters but concluded: 'a boiling kettle of latent violence,' 'compensatory narcissistic fantasy world.'

Masters convicted. 2008 new DNA evidence overturned the conviction; 2011 grand jury dismissed all charges. Meloy later said police had withheld critical evidence; he wouldn't have testified that way.

An innocent man spent nearly a decade in prison.

7 · 各國法院立場比較

🇺🇸

美國

最寬鬆。Daubert 後雖較嚴格，但 profiling 證據（特別由檢方提出）仍常被採信。受 FBI 訓練的探員幾乎自動具備專家資格。George (2008)：「profiling 在美國法庭仍然活躍。」

🇬🇧

英國

Stagg 案後極度懷疑。Gudjonsson & Copson (1997)：90 件審判中只有 2 名 profiler 被認可為專家。Ormerod & Sturman (2005) 呼籲：由 Home Office 認證、嚴格標準、定期審查。

🇨🇦

加拿大

「行為面 profiling 的科學性尚未達到可採性門檻」是 Mohan、Ranger、Klymchuk 三案的一致立場。Canadian Human Rights Commission (2011) 呼籲建立表現標準與研究。

🇦🇺

澳洲

R. v. Hillier (2003) 之後規定：唯有 psychologist 或 psychiatrist 可在法庭提供 profiling 證言。法官名言：「過度產品熱情，就像新車銷售員。」

7 · Country Comparison

🇺🇸

U.S.

Most permissive. Post-Daubert is stricter but profiling evidence (especially from the prosecution) still gets in often. FBI-trained agents nearly auto-qualify. George (2008): 'profiling is alive and well in U.S. courts.'

🇬🇧

UK

Deeply skeptical post-Stagg. Gudjonsson & Copson (1997): only 2 profilers admitted across 90 trials. Ormerod & Sturman (2005) urge Home Office accreditation, strict criteria, periodic review.

🇨🇦

Canada

'Behavioural aspects of profiling have not reached admissibility threshold' — the consistent line of Mohan, Ranger, Klymchuk. The Canadian Human Rights Commission (2011) called for performance standards and research.

🇦🇺

Australia

Post-R. v. Hillier (2003): only psychologists or psychiatrists may give profiling evidence. The judge's line: 'gross product enthusiasm — what we'd call it in a new car salesperson.'

8 · 心理解剖（PA）進法庭

第 8 章已介紹 PA 的科學狀態 — 在法庭採信上反映出同樣的不確定性。

排除 PA 的代表案：R. v. Guilfoyle (UK 2001)

丈夫被控謀殺懷孕妻子（被發現上吊於車庫，留有遺書）。Guilfoyle 在獄中上訴，請 Canter 執行 PA。Canter 結論：妻子確實自殺。

上訴法院裁定：
「心理解剖目前的學術現狀，不足以作為陪審團前的專家意見基礎。」

Canter (2005b) 後承認他當時對「限縮資訊」過度樂觀；但仍堅信 Paula Guilfoyle 是自殺，丈夫被誤判。

允許 PA 的代表案：Jackson v. State (Florida 1989)

17 歲裸舞女自殺；母親偽造其生日讓未成年取得工作 → 被控精神虐待兒童。精神科醫師執行 PA 證明虐待。法院認定：

  • PA 在精神醫學界廣被接受
  • 與「遺囑能力」案件的專家證言類比
  • 即使醫師未訪談死者，仍可信

母親被定罪、上訴維持。

U.S. v. St. Jean (1995)

法院認定「PA 在心理衛生界被接受、對審理者有幫助」。

📌 趨勢觀察：檢方使用 PA 比辯方使用容易採信；民事案件（保險、職場霸凌、員工補償）採信機率高於刑事案件。

8 · Psychological Autopsy in Court

Ch 8 covered PA's scientific status — the courtroom mirror reflects similar uncertainty.

Exclusion: R. v. Guilfoyle (UK 2001)

Husband convicted of murdering his pregnant wife (found hanged in garage with a suicide note). In appeal, Canter performed PA. Conclusion: she truly took her own life.

Court of Appeal:
"The present academic status of psychological autopsies is not, in our judgment, such as to permit them as a basis for expert opinion before a jury."

Canter (2005b) later admitted he was overly optimistic about working with limited access — but still maintains Paula Guilfoyle's suicide and her husband's wrongful conviction.

Admission: Jackson v. State (Florida 1989)

A 17-year-old nude dancer shot herself; her mother had forged her birth certificate to enable underage work → charged with mental child abuse. A psychiatrist's PA proved abuse. The court:

  • PA is accepted in psychiatry
  • Analogous to testamentary capacity expert testimony
  • Reliable even though the psychiatrist never met the decedent

Conviction upheld on appeal.

U.S. v. St. Jean (1995)

Court found PA accepted in the mental health community and helpful to the trier of fact.

📌 Trend: Prosecution PAs are admitted more easily than defense PAs; civil cases (insurance, workplace harassment, workers' comp) admit PA more readily than criminal.

9 · Ultimate Issue Testimony（最終議題證言）

Ultimate Issue

法庭必須做出的「最終決定」 — 此人是否精神錯亂？是否有受審能力？應否判死？傳統上專家證人「不可」直接對此表態，因這是 trier of fact（法官／陪審團）的角色。

Ebisike (2008) 銳利指出：「Ultimate issue 本質上內建於所有 offender profiling 證據中 — 其唯一目的就是指向被告有罪或無罪」。

George (2008) 進一步描述機制：

「檢方的專家先教育陪審團『現場分類學』（含 O/D、動機、人格、行為等），然後檢方再提出被告的證據符合這些特徵 — 因而邀請陪審團得出『被告符合典型兇手 profile』的結論。」

State v. Armstrong (1991)

強姦 8 歲女童案。辯方欲傳臨床心理師證稱「被告心理動力學不符兒童性侵者觀點」 — 檢方主張此實質上是對「無罪」的 ultimate opinion；法院同意，排除該證詞。4th Circuit 上訴法院維持初審判決。

本案展現法院對「以 profile 暗示有罪或無罪」的警覺 — 即使是辯方欲使用。

9 · Ultimate Issue Testimony

Ultimate Issue

The 'final decision' the court must reach — Is this person insane? Competent to stand trial? Should they get the death penalty? Experts traditionally CANNOT opine directly on this — that's the trier of fact's role.

Ebisike (2008): "Ultimate issue is built into all forms of offender profiling evidence... aimed at pointing to the accused's guilt or innocence."

George (2008) on the mechanism:

"A prosecutor's expert educates the jury about crime scene classification, including organized vs disorganized features and how they relate to motivation, personality, and behavior. The prosecutor then submits evidence about the defendant matching these features, inviting the jury to conclude that the defendant meets the profile of a typical offender."

State v. Armstrong (1991)

Rape of an 8-year-old. Defense sought to call a clinical psychologist to testify that 'the defendant's psychodynamics would not support the view of him being a child sexual perpetrator' — prosecution argued this was essentially an ultimate opinion of innocence. The court agreed, excluded the testimony. The 4th Circuit upheld.

This shows the courts guard against 'profile' implying guilt or innocence — even when the defense seeks to use it.

11 · 本章總結

  1. Profiling 進法庭兩條路徑：P-DC（爭議高）vs P-CC（linkage analysis）。
  2. 標準從 Frye (1923) 「通用接受」演化到 Daubert 三部曲：falsifiability + error rate + peer review + general acceptance；Kumho 將之擴及心理學。
  3. 但 95% 法官不真正理解 error rate 與 falsifiability — 實務上仍重「資格」與「通用接受」。
  4. Rachel Nickell 案（R. v. Stagg, 1994）是英國 profiling 的轉折點 — 強烈削弱其法庭採信度。
  5. 現場分析（MO、signature、staging、O/D）證言相對寬容；linkage analysis 在 Pennell 與 Fortin 之間擺盪；動機與人格分析最為法院警惕。
  6. 各國立場：美國最寬鬆、英澳加保守；澳洲限定唯心理 / 精神醫學專業可作證。
  7. PA 在法庭採信高度依司法管轄區而定：UK Guilfoyle 排除、Florida Jackson 接受、St. Jean 接受。
  8. Ultimate issue testimony 為核心爭點 — Ebisike (2008)：「ultimate issue 本質上內建於所有 profiling 證據」。
  9. Drake、Masters 兩案警示我們：自封 profiler 資格驗證不足 + 過度自信證言可能讓無辜者入獄多年。
  10. 支持者建議：嚴格標準化、認證、不應斷言有罪、不應說「符合 / 不符合 profile」；目前提升為 expert knowledge 仍嫌過早。
🎯 一句話帶走：「Profiling 在偵查階段是工具，在審判階段是試金石 — 把這條界線守好。」

11 · Chapter Summary

  1. Two courtroom paths: P-DC (most controversial) vs P-CC (linkage analysis).
  2. Standards evolved from Frye (1923)'s 'general acceptance' to the Daubert trilogy: falsifiability + error rate + peer review + general acceptance; Kumho extended it to psychology.
  3. But ~95% of judges don't really understand error rate and falsifiability — in practice they still lean on qualifications and general acceptance.
  4. The Rachel Nickell case (R. v. Stagg, 1994) was UK profiling's turning point — sharply weakening courtroom acceptance.
  5. Crime scene testimony (MO, signature, staging, O/D) is more tolerated; linkage analysis swings between Pennell and Fortin; motivation/personality is the most wary territory.
  6. Country stances: U.S. most permissive; UK/Canada/Australia conservative; Australia restricts to psychologists/psychiatrists.
  7. PA's courtroom fate varies sharply by jurisdiction: UK Guilfoyle excluded; Florida Jackson admitted; St. Jean admitted.
  8. Ultimate issue is the core debate — Ebisike (2008): 'Ultimate issue is built into all forms of profiling evidence.'
  9. The Drake and Masters cases warn us: insufficient credential verification + overconfident testimony can imprison the innocent for years.
  10. Supporters' recommendations: rigorous standardization, certification, no assertion of guilt, no 'fits/doesn't fit profile' claims; elevation to 'expert knowledge' is premature.
🎯 One-liner takeaway: "Profiling is a tool in the investigation, a touchstone at trial — guard the line between them."
