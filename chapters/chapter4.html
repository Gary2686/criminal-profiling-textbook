1 · Mapping vs Profiling — 一張表分清楚

很多人混用「地理側寫」與「犯罪地圖」這兩個詞。本書（與本章）給它們清楚的分工：

項目Geographic MappingGeographic Profiling
分析對象「眾多」兇手在「一段時間」的犯罪空間「單一」連續犯（或小群體）的空間移動
主要產出Hot spots / hot places 熱點兇手「住處或活動基地」的機率梯度地圖
典型工具GIS、CompStat、GeoDaRigel、CrimeStat、Dragnet
常見用途警力配置、預防、預測警政縮小偵查範圍

但兩者其實互補。Mapping 提供 base rate 與環境理論支援；Profiling 則把這些見解套用到單一案件。

1 · Mapping vs Profiling — One Table

The two terms are often conflated. This chapter (and book) draws a clear distinction:

Geographic MappingGeographic Profiling
SubjectMany offenders over timeOne serial offender (or a small group)
OutputHot spots / hot placesProbability surface for residence / anchor point
ToolsGIS, CompStat, GeoDaRigel, CrimeStat, Dragnet
UsePatrol allocation, prevention, predictive policingNarrowing investigative focus

They're complementary: mapping yields base rates and environmental insight; profiling applies them to a single case.

2 · 從芝加哥學派到環境犯罪學

1833

Gerry 法國區域定罪率差異

Brantingham & Brantingham 認為這是「空間犯罪研究」的起點。

1915

Park & Burgess 芝加哥學派論文

環境犯罪學正式起源；學生繪製「點狀地圖」、訪談、系統觀察。

1920s

Burgess 同心圓理論

城市以同心圓向外擴張，Zone 2「轉型地帶（zone of transition）」犯罪、貧困、健康問題最集中。

1969

Shaw & McKay 驗證

以 100,000+ 名 Cook County 少年法院個案，確認 zone of transition 是「犯罪溫床（hot beds）」。

1970s–80s

環境犯罪學成熟

Brantinghams 強調「犯罪機會分佈」決定犯罪 — 找到「distance decay」現象。

1979

Cohen & Felson — Routine Activity Theory

三要件論：動機犯罪者 + 合適目標 + 缺乏監護者。

1995–2000

Rossmo — CGT → Rigel

加拿大首位「警官+犯罪學博士」，把空間分析變成 GP 演算法。

🏙 為什麼是芝加哥？1900–1930 大移民潮、城市擴張、工業化 → 城市內社會結構快速變動 → 提供觀察「社會解組」與犯罪的天然實驗室。Shaw 後續建立「Chicago Area Project」 — 美國最早的社區行動方案，把學者帶進街頭與少年合作。

2 · From Chicago School to Environmental Criminology

1833

Gerry on French regional conviction rates

Brantinghams call this the start of spatial criminology.

1915

Park & Burgess (Chicago School)

Environmental criminology is formally born; students prepared spot maps, interviews, observations.

1920s

Burgess's Concentric Circle Theory

Cities expand in concentric zones; Zone 2, the "zone of transition," concentrates crime, poverty, and health problems.

1969

Shaw & McKay validation

Using 100,000+ Cook County juveniles, confirmed transition zones as "hot beds" of crime.

1970s–80s

Environmental criminology matures

Brantinghams: opportunity distribution drives crime; the "distance decay" pattern.

1979

Cohen & Felson — Routine Activity Theory

Three elements: motivated offender + suitable target + no capable guardian.

1995–2000

Rossmo — CGT → Rigel

Canada's first cop-PhD turns spatial analysis into a GP algorithm.

🏙 Why Chicago? 1900–1930 immigration, urban expansion, industrialization made the city a natural lab for "social disorganization." Shaw later founded the Chicago Area Project — the first U.S. community-action program putting scholars into streets with youth.

3 · 兩大理論基石

THEORY A

Routine Activity Theory（日常活動理論）

Cohen & Felson (1979) — 原本是「被害學」：為什麼某些人成為被害人？犯罪需要三要件「同時、同地」出現：

動機犯罪者
Motivated offender
+
合適目標
Suitable victim/target
+
缺乏監護者
Absence of guardian

抽掉任何一個都能降低犯罪機率 — 這就是現代犯罪預防的核心邏輯。

THEORY B

Rational Choice Theory（理性選擇理論）

源自 18–19 世紀古典學派（Beccaria、Bentham）；現代版本（Cornish & Clark, 1986）認為犯罪者進行成本效益分析。距離家越遠 → 風險與成本越高 → 必須有更大的預期效益才會去。

這也解釋了為什麼某些連續殺人犯（Dahmer、Gacy、Pickton）把屍體留在家中或家附近 — 運送成本太高。

🧩 Crime Pattern Theory：Brantinghams 把以上兩個理論整合成「犯罪型態理論」 — 環境犯罪學的核心。同時也是地理側寫的理論底座。

3 · Two Foundational Theories

THEORY A

Routine Activity Theory

Cohen & Felson (1979) — originally victimology. Three elements must converge in time and space:

Motivated offender
+
Suitable target
+
Absence of guardian

Remove any one → crime probability drops. The backbone of modern crime prevention.

THEORY B

Rational Choice Theory

Roots in 18th–19th century Classical School (Beccaria, Bentham). Modern form (Cornish & Clark, 1986): offenders perform cost-benefit analysis. Farther from home = higher risk/cost = needs greater expected reward.

It explains why some serial killers (Dahmer, Gacy, Pickton) dispose of bodies at home — transport costs too much.

🧩 Crime Pattern Theory: Brantinghams integrated both into environmental criminology's central framework — also the theoretical bedrock of geographic profiling.

4 · 五個核心地理概念

① Distance Decay 距離衰減

「離兇手住處越遠，犯案機率越低」 — 機率曲線「衰減」。CGT、Rigel、CrimeStat 都建立在這個函數上。Levine (2009) 指出單靠 distance decay 會產生「高度誤差」 — 必須結合其他因素。

② Buffer Zone 緩衝區

兇手住處附近的「不太犯案」區域 — 因為怕被鄰居認出。

  • 典型大小：< ¼ 英里（一般財產犯）
  • 銀行劫匪：~5 英里（銀行少，必須遠走）
  • 汽車竊賊：~1 英里（車多，附近就有）
  • 強姦與命案因衝動性高，buffer zone 不明顯
③ Mental Maps（認知地圖）

Kitchin (1994)：「一個心智裝置，幫我們把無限複雜的世界編碼、簡化、排序。」失智症（Alzheimer's）患者就是失去 mental map 而迷路。兇手的 mental map 包含偏好、價值、可運用機會。Bennett & Wright (1984) 訪南英監獄竊賊發現多數案件有預謀，靠的就是 mental map + cognitive scripts。

④ Awareness / Action Space + Target Backcloth

Brantinghams 三組概念：

  • Action space：兇手日常活動的地理區（家、工作、娛樂、購物、連結路徑）。
  • Awareness space：兇手有「認知地圖」的地理區 — 通常等於 action space。
  • Target backcloth：從兇手角度看「合適目標的時空分佈」 — 隨日期、時段、季節變動。
⑤ Centrality / Domocentricity 中心性

「家就是圓心，犯案散佈於周圍」 — Canter & Larkin (1993) 在英國 87% 連續強姦犯資料中得到驗證。但下節會看到這個假設並不總是成立。

4 · Five Core Geographic Concepts

① Distance Decay

"The farther from the offender's home, the lower the offending probability." CGT, Rigel, CrimeStat all build on this. Levine (2009): relying on decay alone produces high error — combine with other factors.

② Buffer Zone

A nearby zone offenders avoid — fear of being recognized.

  • Typical: < ¼ mile (general property)
  • Bank robbers: ~5 miles (banks are rare, must travel)
  • Car thieves: ~1 mile (cars are everywhere)
  • Rape and homicide — impulsive, buffer less clear
③ Mental Maps

Kitchin (1994): "A mental device and store helping simplify, code, and order the endlessly complex world." Alzheimer's patients lose their mental maps and get lost. Offenders' mental maps include preferences, values, opportunities. Bennett & Wright (1984): most professional burglars planned via mental maps + cognitive scripts.

④ Awareness / Action Space + Target Backcloth

Brantinghams' triad:

  • Action space: routine geographic area (home, work, leisure, shopping, paths).
  • Awareness space: the area for which they have a mental map.
  • Target backcloth: spatial/temporal distribution of suitable targets from the offender's view — varies by time and season.
⑤ Centrality / Domocentricity

"Home is the center; crimes surround it." Canter & Larkin (1993) validated this for 87% of UK serial rapists — but the next section shows it doesn't always hold.

5 · Marauder vs Commuter — 兩種空間人格

🏠 Marauder（劫掠者）
  • ● 從家出發 → 犯案 → 回家
  • ● 犯案地散佈於「家為圓心」的圓圈
  • ● 連續殺人犯：UK 86%、US 89% 為 marauder
  • ● 表達型／情緒型犯罪（強姦、報復縱火）多為 marauder
🚗 Commuter（通勤者）
  • ● 從家「通勤」到另一區犯案
  • ● 犯案地與住處幾乎不重疊
  • ● US 連續強姦犯 51%、加拿大連續性侵犯 43% 為 commuter
  • ● 工具型犯罪（職業搶劫 63% 為 commuter）多為此類
⚠️ Centrality 不是萬靈丹：對某些犯罪類型，centrality 假設只成立約 50%。如果 GP 一開始就假設兇手是 marauder，可能把警力導向錯誤方向。Paulsen (2007) 呼籲：「先預測類型，再做 profile」。

Windshield Wiper Effect 雨刷效應

Lundrigan & Canter (2001) 分析 79 名美國連續殺人犯：兇手不是 360° 均勻散佈，而是像「雨刷」從家為扇柄、平均 60° 扇出。原因：地形（湖、河、高速公路）、社區偏好、交通動線等。

5 · Marauder vs Commuter — Two Spatial Personas

🏠 Marauder
  • ● Leaves home → offends → returns
  • ● Crimes circle the home
  • ● Serial killers: ~86% (UK) / ~89% (US) marauders
  • ● Expressive/emotional crimes (rape, revenge arson) typically marauder
🚗 Commuter
  • ● "Commutes" from home to another area to offend
  • ● Crime sites and home barely overlap
  • ● US serial rapists 51%, Canadian sexual assaulters 43% — commuters
  • ● Instrumental crimes (commercial robbery 63%) often commuters
⚠️ Centrality is not universal: For some crime types, it holds only ~50%. Assuming marauder upfront can misdirect police. Paulsen (2007): "First predict the type, then build the profile."

The Windshield Wiper Effect

Lundrigan & Canter (2001), 79 U.S. serial killers: offenders don't spread 360° evenly — they fan out like a windshield wiper at ~60° average, from home as the pivot. Reasons: terrain (lakes, rivers, highways), neighborhood preference, transport.

6 · Rossmo 狩獵類型（4 × 3）

Rossmo (1997) 把「掠食者」式連續犯依「找受害者方式」與「攻擊方式」分為兩個正交維度：

四種狩獵模式

🏹

Hunter 獵人

從家出發、在 awareness space 內主動找受害者；地理穩定。≈ marauder。

🦌

Poacher 盜獵者

較流動，遠離家去找。≈ commuter。

🚶

Troller 漫遊者

不刻意找；在日常活動中偶遇受害者。

🕸

Trapper 設陷阱者

創造情境讓受害者上門 — 約會、登廣告、收房客、特定職位。多數女性連續殺人犯（「黑寡婦」、「死亡天使」、機構照顧者）屬此。

三種攻擊方式

方式定義
Raptor 猛禽相遇即攻擊
Stalker 潛行先尾隨後攻擊
Ambusher 伏擊誘騙到自己控制地點才下手；屍體常藏在自宅
💡 GP 對哪些組合最有效？ Hunter/Raptor 和 Trapper/Ambusher 是最常見組合，但 Poacher 和 Stalker 通常「不會產生適合 GP 的目標模式」。

6 · Rossmo's Hunting Typology (4 × 3)

Rossmo (1997) classifies predatory serial offenders on two orthogonal dimensions:

Four Hunting Patterns

🏹

Hunter

Sets out from home; searches awareness space for victims; geographically stable. ≈ marauder.

🦌

Poacher

More transient; travels far from home. ≈ commuter.

🚶

Troller

No specific search — randomly encounters victims during routine activity.

🕸

Trapper

Creates situations to draw victims — dates, want ads, boarders, certain jobs. Most female serial killers (black widows, angels of death, custodial killers) fit here.

Three Attack Methods

MethodDefinition
RaptorAttack on encounter
StalkerFollow then attack
AmbusherLure to a controlled location; bodies often hidden on the offender's property
💡 Which combinations suit GP best? Hunter/Raptor and Trapper/Ambusher are most common; Poacher and Stalker typically don't produce target patterns amenable to GP.

7 · 三大 GP 軟體

軟體開發者核心邏輯特性
Rigel Kim Rossmo（基於 CGT 演算法） 3D 機率地圖；計算每個區域是兇手基地的機率 商業軟體、功能最齊全；可自動產生 search area、報告、嫌犯優先順序
CrimeStat Ned Levine（NIJ 資助） Crime travel demand model；中心最小距離 Windows、整合多數 GIS；v3.x 起加入 5 種 distance decay 函數與 Bayesian
Dragnet David Canter（UK） 互動式機率指標 輕量、PC 操作門檻低；同時可做研究工具

常見準確度指標

  • Error distance：實際住處到預測最高機率區的距離。
  • Search cost / Hit score：要搜索多大比例的格子才會找到兇手基地。
  • Top profile area：模型預測最可能是基地的區塊大小。
  • Profile accuracy：兇手是否落在 top profile area 內。

Accuracy Metrics

  • Error distance: actual residence vs. predicted top-probability area.
  • Search cost / hit score: what fraction of grid must be searched.
  • Top profile area: predicted area most likely to contain the base.
  • Profile accuracy: does the offender fall inside the top profile area?
FOCUS 4.1

ATF and GP

Since 1986 the U.S. ATF has assigned agents to NCAVC — currently one behavioral profiler + one GP profiler. ATF is the only U.S. federal agency with a GP profiler. Two-year training, ICIAF-certified.

8 · GP 六個基本假設（不滿足就會出錯）

  1. 同一兇手：一系列案件必須能連結到同一人。
  2. ≥ 3–4 件：太少無法穩定連結。Knabe-Nicol & Alison (2011) 證明單一多地點案件仍可用 GP，但精度大降。
  3. 案件散佈廣 + 有中央位置：太集中無法區分基地。
  4. 穩定的單一基地：Beltway Sniper 案中 Muhammad & Malvo 住車內、汽車旅館、熟人家 — 直接違反這條，GP 無能為力。
  5. 案件間隔短（週、月）：時間太長警方無法初步連結。
  6. 案件連續發生（非同日）：同日多案無法反映兇手「回基地→再出擊」的時間結構。
📉 Beltway Sniper 為何 GP 失敗？違反假設 4（無穩定基地）+ 跨州（馬里蘭→維吉尼亞）。若不是媒體大量報導+其特殊 MO，可能會落入 Harries & LeBeau 警告的「跨轄區、無人協調」最糟劇本。

8 · GP's Six Basic Assumptions

  1. One offender: cases linkable to a single person.
  2. ≥ 3–4 cases: too few prevents stable linking. Knabe-Nicol & Alison (2011) show GP can help single multi-location crimes, with reduced precision.
  3. Wide dispersal with center: too clustered defeats anchor estimation.
  4. Single stable base: Beltway Snipers slept in car/motels/acquaintances' homes — broke this and GP failed.
  5. Short intervals (weeks/months): too long blocks initial linking.
  6. Continuous series (not same day): same-day events don't reflect the "return to base → strike" rhythm.
📉 Why GP failed for Beltway: violated #4 + cross-state (MD→VA). Without media saturation and a dramatic MO, it could have hit the Harries & LeBeau "worst case" — uncoordinated multi-jurisdiction.

10 · Bayesian 方法的反擊

面對「簡單捷思法贏過電腦」的挑戰，電腦派加入 Empirical Bayes 方法。CrimeStat 3.1 新版產生 3 個風險面：

  1. 傳統 journey-to-crime / distance decay
  2. 過往兇手住處分布（與案發地無關）
  3. 「Origin–destination zone matrix」 — 把移動成本、特定吸引點、地理障礙都納入

Block & Bernasco (2009) 在 62 名竊賊資料中證實：Bayesian > 傳統 distance decay，對 marauder 效果最好。

Leitner & Kent (2009) 用 Baltimore County 850 件「多類型犯罪系列」（如 2 竊盜 + 3 車竊 + 1 縱火）發現：整合不同犯罪類型反而比同類型更準 — 因為多類型系列中 marauder 比例更高。挑戰「同類型才能做 GP」的傳統做法。

10 · The Bayesian Counterattack

Facing the heuristics challenge, software teams added Empirical Bayes. CrimeStat 3.1 produces 3 risk surfaces:

  1. Classic journey-to-crime / distance decay
  2. Past-offender residence distribution (independent of where they offended)
  3. "Origin–destination zone matrix" — incorporates travel cost, attractions, barriers

Block & Bernasco (2009) on 62 burglars: Bayesian > traditional decay, best for marauders.

Leitner & Kent (2009) used Baltimore County's 850 multi-crime-type series (e.g., 2 burglaries + 3 auto thefts + 1 arson) — integrating types improved accuracy: higher marauder proportions in multi-type series. Challenges the "same-type only" tradition.

12 · 本章總結

  1. Mapping 看群體，Profiling 看個別連續犯。
  2. 芝加哥學派的同心圓 + Shaw & McKay 的少年犯資料奠定空間犯罪學。
  3. Routine Activity Theory 與 Rational Choice Theory 是 GP 的理論雙柱。
  4. 五個核心概念（distance decay、buffer zone、mental maps、awareness space、centrality）是讀 GP 報告的入場券。
  5. Canter & Larkin 的 Marauder/Commuter 二分 + Rossmo 的 Hunter/Poacher/Troller/Trapper 四分 + Raptor/Stalker/Ambusher 三攻擊法，提供更細的空間人格畫像。
  6. Rigel / CrimeStat / Dragnet 三大軟體基於 distance decay；但 Snook、Bennell 等的實驗顯示「兩個簡單 heuristic」就能達到甚至超過軟體準確度。
  7. Bayesian 改進方法（CrimeStat 3.1）整合移動成本、地理障礙、過往住處 → 對 marauder 最有效。
  8. 六個基本假設未滿足時（Beltway Sniper 案）GP 會失效。
🎯 一句話帶走：「兇手會用兩條腿和方向盤洩漏他的祕密 — 只要你看懂他的 mental map。」

12 · Chapter Summary

  1. Mapping looks at populations; profiling at individual serial offenders.
  2. The Chicago School's concentric circles + Shaw & McKay's juvenile data founded spatial criminology.
  3. Routine Activity + Rational Choice are GP's twin theoretical pillars.
  4. Five core concepts (distance decay, buffer zone, mental maps, awareness space, centrality) are entry tickets to GP reports.
  5. Canter & Larkin's Marauder/Commuter + Rossmo's Hunter/Poacher/Troller/Trapper + Raptor/Stalker/Ambusher add finer spatial-personality detail.
  6. Rigel / CrimeStat / Dragnet rely on distance decay — but Snook, Bennell et al. show two simple heuristics can match or beat them.
  7. Bayesian improvements (CrimeStat 3.1) integrate travel cost, barriers, past residences — best for marauders.
  8. When the six assumptions fail (Beltway Sniper), GP collapses.
🎯 One-liner takeaway: "An offender betrays himself with his legs and his steering wheel — if you can read his mental map."
